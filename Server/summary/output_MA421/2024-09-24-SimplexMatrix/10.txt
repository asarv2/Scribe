\textbf{Description:}

This slide presents a tabular representation of the simplex method's matrix operations.  The table shows the components needed to update the objective function and the basic variables during a simplex iteration.  The top row represents the constant term ($\overline{\mathcal{Z}}$) and the coefficients of the non-basic variables in the objective function.  The bottom row represents the values of the basic variables ($\mathbf{x}_B$) and the coefficients of the non-basic variables in the constraint equations.  The table is organized to highlight the matrix operations involved in updating the simplex tableau.  The notation $B$, $N$, $c_B$, $c_N$, and $B^{-1}$ are all matrix representations used in the simplex algorithm.  This slide is likely part of a lecture explaining the simplex method's underlying structure and how the tableau is updated during iterations.
