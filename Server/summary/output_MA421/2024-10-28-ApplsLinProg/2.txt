\textbf{Description:}

This slide continues the previous example of Icicle Works Ltd.  It presents the same bar graph of predicted monthly ice cream sales.  However, a new element is added: a horizontal red line has been drawn across the graph at approximately 500 tons.  This line represents the average demand for ice cream throughout the year.  The text "average demand" is written below the line, indicating that this is a key metric for the production planning.  The slide is likely part of a lecture or presentation explaining how to use linear programming to create a production plan based on predicted demand and average demand.
