\textbf{Example in $\mathbb{R}^3$}

3 x 3 system:
\begin{align*}
\begin{cases}
x + 2y + z = 1 \\
3x + y + 4z = 0 \\
2x + 2y + 3z = 2
\end{cases}
\end{align*}

Each equation represents a plane in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
