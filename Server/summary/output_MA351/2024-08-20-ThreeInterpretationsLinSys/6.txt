\textbf{(III)} Linear Transformation of Vectors

Solving
\begin{align*}
\begin{cases}
2x - y = 4 \\
x + 3y = 9
\end{cases}
\end{align*}
is equivalent to finding $(x, y)$ such that $T(x, y) = (4, 9)$.
(i.e. $(4, 9)$ is the image of $(x, y)$ under $T$).
