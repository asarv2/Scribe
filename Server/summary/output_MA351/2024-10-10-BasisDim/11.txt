Slide 11

Properties of $dim(V)$

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Any list of $m$ vectors of $V$, $m > dim(V)$, must be linearly dependent.  Then $m \ge dim(V)$.
    \item The maximum number of linearly independent vectors in $V$ is $dim(V)$.
    \item The minimum number of vectors to span $V$ is $dim(V)$.
    \item Any list of $dim(V)$ linearly independent vectors spans $V$.
\end{enumerate}
