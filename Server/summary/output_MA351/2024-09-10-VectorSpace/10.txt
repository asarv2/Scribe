\section*{(General) Vector Space $V$}

\textbf{Examples of (General) Vector Space}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item $P_n = \{polynomials \ of \ degree \ at \ most \ n\}$
    \newline
    = $\{p(x) = a_0 + a_1 x + a_2 x^2 + ... + a_n x^n\}$
    \newline
    $p_1(x) = a_0 + a_1 x + a_2 x^2 + ... + a_n x^n$
    \newline
    $p_2(x) = b_0 + b_1 x + b_2 x^2 + ... + b_n x^n$
    \newline
    $(p_1 + p_2)(x) = (a_0 + b_0) + (a_1 + b_1) x + ... + (a_n + b_n) x^n$
\end{enumerate}

--- Description ---

This slide provides examples of vector spaces.  It specifically shows $P_n$, the set of polynomials of degree at most $n$.  The example demonstrates how vector addition works in this context.  The sum of two polynomials $p_1(x)$ and $p_2(x)$ results in a new polynomial $(p_1 + p_2)(x)$ where the coefficients of corresponding powers of $x$ are added.
