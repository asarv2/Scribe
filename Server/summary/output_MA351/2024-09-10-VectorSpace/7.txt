\section*{Linear Combination and Span}

Given $\mathcal{L} = \{\vec{u}_1, \vec{u}_2, ..., \vec{u}_k\} \subseteq R^n$.

\textbf{Main Question:}
\newline
Given a vector $\vec{v} \in R^n$, determine if $\vec{v} \in Span\{\vec{u}_1, ..., \vec{u}_k\}$.
\newline
Find $c_1, c_2, ..., c_k$ such that
\newline
$\vec{v} = c_1 \vec{u}_1 + c_2 \vec{u}_2 + ... + c_k \vec{u}_k$
