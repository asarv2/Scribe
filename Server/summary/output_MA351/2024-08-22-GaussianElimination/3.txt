$\begin{bmatrix}

a_{31} & a_{32} & \dots & a_{3n} & | & b_3 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & | & \vdots \\

a_{m1} & a_{m2} & \dots & a_{mn} & | & b_m
\end{bmatrix}$

\textit{Coefficient matrix} $\quad$ \textit{Augmented matrix}

\textit{Description:}

This slide takes the system of linear equations from the previous slides and represents them in matrix form.  It shows the coefficient matrix (the $m \times n$ matrix containing the coefficients $a_{ij}$) and the augmented matrix (the $m \times (n+1)$ matrix formed by adding the constant terms $b_i$ to the coefficient matrix).  The vertical bar separates the coefficient matrix from the constant terms.  This representation is crucial for applying Gaussian elimination, as it allows for systematic manipulation of the rows of the augmented matrix to solve the system.  The slide is part of a larger presentation likely covering Gaussian elimination and its application to linear programming problems.  The terms "coefficient matrix" and "augmented matrix" are highlighted, emphasizing the key components of the matrix representation of the system of equations.
