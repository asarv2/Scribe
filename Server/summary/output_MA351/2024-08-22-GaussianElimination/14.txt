Slide 14

Examples: Express pivot var. in terms of free var.

(b)
\begin{align*}
\begin{cases}
x + 2y + 3z + 3w = 0 \\
z - w = 7 \\
\end{cases}
\end{align*}

$\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 & 3 & | & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & -1 & | & 7
\end{bmatrix}$

$w = s$ (free)
$z = w + 7 = s + 7$
$x = -2y - 3z - 3w = -2y - 3(s + 7) - 3s = -2y - 3s - 21 - 3s = -2y - 6s - 21$
$y = t$ (free)

Solution:
$\begin{bmatrix}
x \\
y \\
z \\
w
\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
-21 - 6s - 2t \\
t \\
7 + s \\
s
\end{bmatrix}$
