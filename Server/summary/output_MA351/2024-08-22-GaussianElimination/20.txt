Slide 20

(3) No Solution (inconsistent)

In RREF, no. of non-zero rows in the coefficient matrix is less than the no. of non-zero rows in the augmented matrix.

$\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 1 & | & * \\
0 & 1 & | & * \\
0 & 0 & | & *
\end{bmatrix}$

\textit{Description:}

This slide summarizes the scenario of an inconsistent system of linear equations when using Gaussian elimination and Reduced Row Echelon Form (RREF).  It states that a system is inconsistent if, after transforming the augmented matrix to RREF, the number of non-zero rows in the coefficient matrix is less than the number of non-zero rows in the augmented matrix.  The slide provides a visual representation of an example of such an inconsistent system.  The asterisks (*) represent arbitrary numbers.  This scenario indicates that there is no solution to the system of equations.  This slide is part of a larger presentation likely covering Gaussian elimination and its application to linear programming problems.  The concept of an inconsistent system is crucial for understanding the limitations of solving systems of linear equations.
