\textbf{Description:}

This slide continues the discussion of matrix composition.  It defines the matrix $AB$ as a collection of vectors, each being the result of multiplying matrix $A$ by the corresponding column vector $B_i$ of matrix $B$.  This is a crucial step in understanding how matrix multiplication represents the composition of linear transformations.  The notation and context strongly suggest this is part of a linear algebra lecture, likely leading into a discussion of linear programming, where matrix operations are fundamental for defining and solving problems.  The slide emphasizes the relationship between the columns of the product matrix $AB$ and the columns of matrix $B$.
