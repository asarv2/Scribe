\textbf{Properties of the Simplex Method}

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Linearity:} The objective function and constraints must be linear.
    \item \textbf{Non-negativity:} All variables must be non-negative.
    \item \textbf{Bounded Feasible Region:} The feasible region must be bounded.
    \item \textbf{Optimality:} The simplex method finds the optimal solution if one exists.
    \item \textbf{Iteration:} The method iteratively improves the solution until the optimal solution is found.
\end{itemize}
