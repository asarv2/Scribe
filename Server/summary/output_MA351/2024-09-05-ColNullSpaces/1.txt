\textbf{Description:}

This slide introduces the concept of column space and null space in the context of a linear system.  It shows a $m \times n$ linear system, represented by a matrix equation $Ax = b$, where $A$ is the coefficient matrix, $x$ is the vector of unknowns, and $b$ is the vector of constants. The slide visually depicts the column space (Col(A)) as the span of the columns of the matrix $A$, and the null space (Null(A)) as the set of all vectors $x$ that satisfy $Ax = 0$.  The slide also shows the relationship between the unknowns ($x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$) and the constants ($b_1, b_2, \dots, b_m$).  The arrows visually represent the relationships between the variables and the constants.  This is the first slide of a 25-slide presentation on linear programming, likely introducing the fundamental concepts of linear systems.
