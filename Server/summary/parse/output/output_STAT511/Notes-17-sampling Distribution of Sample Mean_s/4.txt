Example 17.3 The amount of a particular impurity in a batch of a certain chemical product is a random variable with mean value 4.0 g and standard deviation 1.5 g.

1) What is the (approximate) probability that the amount of a particular impurity X is between 3.5 and 3.8 g?

2) If 50 batches are independently prepared, what is the (approximate) probability that the sample average amount of impurity X̄ is between 3.5 and 3.8 g?

X̄ has approximately a normal distribution with mean μ<sub>X̄</sub> = μ = 4.0 and σ<sub>X̄</sub> = σ/√n = 0.2121.

P(3.5 ≤ X̄ ≤ 3.8) ≈ P( (3.5 - 4.0)/0.2121 ≤ Z ≤ (3.8 - 4.0)/0.2121 ) = Φ(-0.94) - Φ(-2.36) = 0.1645
