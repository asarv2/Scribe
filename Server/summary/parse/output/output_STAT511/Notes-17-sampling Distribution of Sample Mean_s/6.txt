**Distribution of Linear Combination**

Yuan Qu

In this section, we consider the statistics: given fixed coefficients a<sub>i</sub>'s

Y = a<sub>1</sub>X<sub>1</sub> + ... + a<sub>n</sub>X<sub>n</sub>

Note sample mean is a special case where a<sub>i</sub> = 1/n.


Proposition: Let X<sub>i</sub> be a random variable with mean μ<sub>i</sub> and variance σ<sub>i</sub><sup>2</sup>. Then,

1. E(Y) = E(a<sub>1</sub>X<sub>1</sub> + ... + a<sub>n</sub>X<sub>n</sub>) = a<sub>1</sub>E(X<sub>1</sub>) + ... + a<sub>n</sub>E(X<sub>n</sub>) = a<sub>1</sub>μ<sub>1</sub> + ... + a<sub>n</sub>μ<sub>n</sub>.

2. If X<sub>i</sub> are mutually independent rv's, then

Var(Y) = a<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup>Var(X<sub>1</sub>) + ... + a<sub>n</sub><sup>2</sup>Var(X<sub>n</sub>) = a<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup>σ<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup> + ... + a<sub>n</sub><sup>2</sup>σ<sub>n</sub><sup>2</sup>.

3. In general

Var(a<sub>1</sub>X<sub>1</sub> + ... + a<sub>n</sub>X<sub>n</sub>) = Σ<sub>i=1</sub><sup>n</sup> Σ<sub>j=1</sub><sup>n</sup> a<sub>i</sub>a<sub>j</sub>Cov(X<sub>i</sub>, X<sub>j</sub>) = Σ<sub>i=1</sub><sup>n</sup> a<sub>i</sub><sup>2</sup>Var(X<sub>i</sub>) + 2 Σ<sub>i<j</sub> Cov(X<sub>i</sub>, X<sub>j</sub>).


Note: The above proposition concerns only mean and variance, but not the distribution. In general, the distribution shape of a linear combination is not easy to figure out.


Usually, a linear combination of normal variable is also a normal random variable. Since a normal distribution is completely determined by its mean and variance, we only need to figure out the distribution of a linear combination of normal rv's.


The difference between two random variables

An important special case of a linear combination results from taking n = 2, a<sub>1</sub> = 1, and a<sub>2</sub> = -1. We get the formula for the different of two rv's.

Proposition:

1. E(X<sub>1</sub> - X<sub>2</sub>) = E(X<sub>1</sub>) - E(X<sub>2</sub>) = μ<sub>1</sub> - μ<sub>2</sub> for any two rv's.

2. Var(X<sub>1</sub> - X<sub>2</sub>) = Var(X<sub>1</sub>) + Var(X<sub>2</sub>) = σ<sub>1</sub><sup>2</sup> + σ<sub>2</sub><sup>2</sup> if X<sub>1</sub> and X<sub>2</sub> are independent rv's.
