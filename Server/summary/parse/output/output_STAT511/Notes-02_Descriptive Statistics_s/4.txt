**Description of the Slide:**

This slide, part of a Statistical Methods lecture, focuses on describing the shape of numerical data distributions.  It introduces the concepts of modality (the number of peaks or modes in a distribution) and skewness (the asymmetry of a distribution).  The slide presents examples of unimodal, bimodal, and uniform distributions, along with examples of symmetric, skewed right, and skewed left distributions.  Each type of distribution is illustrated with a corresponding histogram.  The slide also includes a question (Example 2.3) prompting the students to analyze the shape of different data sets, identifying both the modality and skewness.  The various histograms are crucial for visually demonstrating the different shapes and characteristics of data distributions.  The examples are designed to help students develop their ability to recognize and describe the shape of a distribution.
