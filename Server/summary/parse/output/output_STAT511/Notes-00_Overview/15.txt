**Homework**

Unless announced otherwise on Brightspace, homework will be due on
WebAssign before midnight (11:59 PM) on Fridays, starting in Week 2.

* A special assignment (Homework 0) for the 1st week is posted for
extra credit 1%.
* 12 regular homework assignments worth 40~90 points each.
* 1 lowest HW score dropped (out of 12) automatically at the end of
semester.

- The dropped one is intended for when you are ill, out of town, or just plain
need a break, whatever the reason. Don't come to me after you've already
"dropped" one and ask for another!
