**Academic Integrity**

OK to discuss the problems with others, but your work should be your own.
- Know difference between good collaboration and academic misconduct.

Please do not cheat or copy anything!

- Copying will NOT help you learn.
Cheating results in an F grade in this course, immediate report to Office of Dean of Students for adjudication.
