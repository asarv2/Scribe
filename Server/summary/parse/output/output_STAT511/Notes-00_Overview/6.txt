**What You Can Expect from Me**

*   Provide you with the standard data analysis tools.
*   Engage with you; guide you in your statistical thinking.
*   Critique ideas, but not you; respect that you are learning.

*   TA and I will hold office hours to help with assignments and all questions related to this course.
