**Suggested Software**

We do not require specific computer software for this course. Instead,
students are expected to know how to do the analysis and calculation
without using existing packages. NO computer is available for exams.
Excel would be adequate for homework assignments. R, SAS, MATLAB,
SPSS and other software will also be fine.
R is suggested since it’s a free software environment for statistical
computing and graphics. It also compiles and runs on a wide variety of
UNIX platforms, Windows and MacOS.
http://www.r-project.org/index.html
