**Five Number Summary & Outliers**

* **Five Number Summary**
    * Minimum
    * 1st quartile (lower fourth)
    * Median
    * 3rd quartile (upper fourth)
    * Maximum

* **Outliers:** The formal definition of an outlier is any observation that meets one of the following criteria:
    * Any observation farther than 1.5 × IQR from the closest fourth is an outlier/mild outlier, that is, any data value that is less than Q₁ − 1.5 × IQR or greater than Q₃ + 1.5 × IQR.
    * An extreme outlier is any data value that is smaller than Q₁ − 3 × IQR or greater than Q₃ + 3 × IQR.
    * Extreme outliers are frequently influential points whereas an outlier that isn’t also an extreme outlier may not be an influential point.


**Boxplots: Graphical Versions of the Five Number Summary**

* Box plots can be used to identify **outliers** in the data.
