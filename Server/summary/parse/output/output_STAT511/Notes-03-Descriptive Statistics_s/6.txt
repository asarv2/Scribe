**Example 3.5:**

**Figure Descriptions:**

* **Multiple Histograms:**  There are two histograms, positioned side-by-side.  The x-axis of each histogram represents the range of values (likely sodium or sugar content in cereal). The y-axis represents the count or frequency of data points within each bin.  The histograms are used to visually compare the distributions of sugar and sodium content in cereal samples.

* **Box Plots:** There are three box plots, positioned below the histograms.  Each box plot represents a different group (active smokers, passive smokers, nonsmokers). The box spans from the first quartile (Q1) to the third quartile (Q3), with a line inside representing the median. Whiskers extend from the box to the minimum and maximum values within 1.5 times the interquartile range (IQR).  Dots or other symbols outside the whiskers indicate outliers.  The box plots are used to compare the distributions of cotinine levels across the three groups.

* **Overall:** The figures are used to illustrate the comparison of data distributions using both box plots and histograms.  The histograms show the overall distribution, while the box plots highlight the central tendency and variability within each group.  The side-by-side arrangement of the box plots facilitates direct comparison of the distributions across the different groups.
