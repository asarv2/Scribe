Example 20.5 A data set of 30 sweetgum lumbers modulus rupture gives the sample mean and sample standard deviation 7203.191 and 543.5400 and a normal probability plot as:

Q: calculate a confidence interval for true average MOR using a confidence level of 95%.

The CI is based on n – 1 = 29 degrees of freedom, so the necessary t critical value is t<sub>0.025,29</sub> = 2.045.
The interval estimate is

𝑥̅ ± 𝑡<sub>0.025,29</sub> ∙𝑠/√𝑛 = 7203.191 ± (2.045) ∙543.5400/√30

= 7203.191 ± 202.938

We estimate 7000.253 < μ < 7406.129 that with 95% confidence.
