**Example 20.3:** The FFQ is a food frequency questionnaire, which uses a carefully designed series of questions to determine the dietary intakes of participants in the study.

In the Nurses' Health Study, a random sample of 166 female nurses each completed the FFQ. The variable being measured is the % fat in the diet.  𝑥̅ = 36.50 and s = 6.75.

Q1: find the approximated confidence intervals for μ = average% fat in the diet of all women similar to the female nurses in this study during the time period that this study was conducted.

90% CI for μ:  
𝑥̅ ± 1.645 ∙  6.75 / √166 = 36.5 ± 0.86

Q2: what happens to the margin of error as the confidence level increases?

Q3: what is the correct interpretation of the 90% confidence interval we calculated?
