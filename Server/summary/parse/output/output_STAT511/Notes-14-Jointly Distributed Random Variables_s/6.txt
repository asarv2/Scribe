The deductible amount of automobile (X) and homeowner (Y) insurance policies:

|       | y = 0 | y = 100 | y = 200 | p<sub>X</sub>(x) |
|-------|--------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| x = 100 | 0.20  | 0.10     | 0.20     | 0.50      |
| x = 250 | 0.05  | 0.15     | 0.30     | 0.50      |
| p<sub>Y</sub>(y) | 0.25  | 0.25     | 0.50     |          |

P(X = 100, Y = 200) = 0.20 ≠ (0.5)(0.5) = p<sub>X</sub>(100) × p<sub>Y</sub>(200), so X and Y are not independent.

**Note:** In fact, independence of X and Y requires that every entry (the joint probability) be the product of the corresponding row and column marginal probabilities.
