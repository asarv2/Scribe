**Multiple Random Variables**

In the last course notes, we only consider the case of one single random variable. In this note, we consider the case where there are multiple random variables. We aim to understand how to study the relationship among these random variables.

**Probability distribution of two discrete random variables**

Assume that there are two random variables, X and Y, and both of them are Discrete and defined on the sample space S of an experiment.

**Joint Probability Mass function p(x, y)** is defined for each pair of numbers (x, y) by:

p(x, y) = P(X = x and Y = y)

For any set A which is a subset of R², its associated probability is

P((X, Y) ∈ A) = ΣΣ_{(x,y)∈A} p(x, y)

**Note:** joint probability is a legitimate probability mass function defined on 2-dimensional real space. All the probability axioms still hold.
