**Sample percentiles**
Order the *n* sample observations from smallest to largest. Then the *i*<sup>th</sup> smallest observation in the list is taken to be the [100(i – 0.5)/n]<sup>th</sup> sample percentile.

**Population percentiles**

If the sample was actually selected from the specified distribution, the sample percentiles (ordered sample observations) should be reasonably close to the corresponding population distribution percentiles.

Let's consider the (population percentile, sample percentile) pairs — that is, the pairs ([100(i – 0.5)/n]<sup>th</sup> percentile of the distribution, *i*<sup>th</sup> smallest sample observation) for *i* = 1, …, *n*.
