**z<sub>α</sub> notation for z Critical Values**

In statistical inference, we will need the values on the horizontal z axis that capture certain small tail areas under the standard normal curve.

**Notation**

z<sub>α</sub> will denote the value on the z axis for which α of the area under the z curve lies to the right of z<sub>α</sub>.

* For example, z<sub>0.10</sub> captures upper-tail area 0.10, and z<sub>0.01</sub> captures upper-tail area 0.01.

Since α of the area under the z curve lies to the right of z<sub>α</sub>, 1 – α of the area lies to its left.

Thus z<sub>α</sub> is the 100(1 – α)th percentile of the standard normal distribution.

By symmetry the area under the standard normal curve to the left of –z<sub>α</sub> is also α. The z<sub>α</sub>'s are usually referred to as z critical values.
