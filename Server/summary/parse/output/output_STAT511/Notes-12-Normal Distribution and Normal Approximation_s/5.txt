**Slide Description:**

This slide continues the examples from the previous slides, demonstrating how to calculate probabilities and proportions for normally distributed data.  It focuses on finding the standard deviation (σ) given a desired probability within a certain range of the mean (µ) and calculating probabilities for different ranges of values relative to the mean and standard deviation.  The examples are practical applications of the concepts of normal distributions and z-scores.  The slide uses the empirical rule as an alternative method for approximating the solutions.
