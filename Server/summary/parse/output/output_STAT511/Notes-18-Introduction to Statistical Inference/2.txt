**Description of the Slide:**

This slide presents four histograms (a, b, c, and d) representing different scenarios of bias and variability in estimating a population parameter.  Each histogram is labeled with a letter (a, b, c, or d) and is accompanied by a corresponding number (1, 2, 3, or 4) that corresponds to a description of the bias and variability.  The histograms are used to visually illustrate the concepts of bias (how far the center of the distribution is from the true value) and variability (how spread out the distribution is).  The slide is designed to help students understand the relationship between the shape of a distribution and the characteristics of bias and variability.
