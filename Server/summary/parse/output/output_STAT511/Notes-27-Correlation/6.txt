**Properties of 𝑟**

* The value of 𝑟 does not depend on which of the two variables under study is labeled 𝑥 and which is labeled 𝑦.
* The value of 𝑟 is independent of the units in which 𝑥 and 𝑦 are measured.
* – 1 ≤𝑟≤1 or 𝑟∈[−1,1]
* 𝑟 is positive mean positive linear relationship and vice versa;
* Absolute value of 𝑟 measure the strength of linear relationship.
