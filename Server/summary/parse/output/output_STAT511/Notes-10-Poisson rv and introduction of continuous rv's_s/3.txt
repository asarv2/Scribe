**Statistical Methods**

We calculate probabilities by finding areas under the density curve:

P(a ≤ X ≤ b) = the area under the density curve between a and b.

For f(x) to be a legitimate pdf, it must satisfy the following two conditions:

1. f(x) ≥ 0 for all x
2. ∫<sub>-∞</sub><sup>∞</sup> f(x) dx = area under the entire graph of f(x) = 1

Handy facts for continuous random variables:

* P(X = c) for any number c.
* P(X ≤ c) = P(X < c).
