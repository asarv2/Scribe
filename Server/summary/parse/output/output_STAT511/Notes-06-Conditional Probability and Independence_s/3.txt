**General Formula:** When P(B) > 0, the conditional probability of A given B is

P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / P(B)

• A Venn diagram illuminates the relationship of the conditional probability and P(A ∩ B) and P(B).
• Given that B has occurred, the relevant sample space is no longer S, but consists of outcomes in B; A has occurred if and only if one of the outcomes in the intersection occurred, so the conditional probability of A given B is proportional to P(A ∩ B).
• The proportionality constant 1/P(B) is used to ensure that the probability P(B | B) of the new sample space B equals 1.
