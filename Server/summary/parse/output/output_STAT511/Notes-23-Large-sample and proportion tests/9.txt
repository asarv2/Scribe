Statistical significance in practical uses

* Practical significance relates to whether a result from a statistical hypothesis test is useful in real life. Statistical significance is just one piece of the puzzle when it comes to assessing practical significance.

* Statistical significance means the null hypothesis:  H<sub>0</sub>: μ = μ<sub>0</sub> is rejected. Statistical significance may not mean practical difference from μ<sub>0</sub>. If we increase the sample size n, any small departure from H<sub>0</sub> will be detected, which may not be practically important.
