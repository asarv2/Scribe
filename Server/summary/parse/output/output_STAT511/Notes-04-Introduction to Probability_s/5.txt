**Description of the Slide:**

This slide continues the Statistical Methods lecture, focusing on applying probability concepts to a real-world example.  The top portion of the slide presents two probability problems (b and c) related to selecting faculty members based on their names and teaching experience.  These problems require calculating probabilities based on given data.  The problems are set up to be solved using the principles of probability, including the concept of mutually exclusive events and calculating probabilities from a sample space.  The slide then introduces a general method for constructing probabilities when the sample space is countable.  The example (Example 4.5) uses a medical study (Booth et al, 2003) to illustrate the application of probability to a dataset.  A table is included, showing the proportion of patients with different symptoms of SARS.  The problems on the slide are designed to be solved using the information provided in the table.  The slide is structured to provide a practical application of the concepts introduced in previous slides.
