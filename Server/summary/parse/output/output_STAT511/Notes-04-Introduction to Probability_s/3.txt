* **Basic Probability Rules**

* **Interpretation of probability**
    * P(A) can be interpreted as a long-term frequency of occurrence of the event A, when the experiment is replicated many times.
    * P(A) = # of times A occurs / # of times chance operation is repeated.

* **Equally likely outcomes** - A classic probability definition with equally likely outcomes.
    * P(A) = # of elements in A / # of elements in the whole sample space.
