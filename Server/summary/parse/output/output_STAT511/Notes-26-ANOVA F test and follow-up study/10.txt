**Statistical Methods**

**The Tukey's Method**

The Tukey's procedure is an alternative method to solve the difficulty of controlling the simultaneous confidence level for all (I(I-1))/2 intervals.

Proposition: With probability 1 - α, the confidence interval for µ<sub>i</sub> - µ<sub>j</sub> is

(𝑋̅<sub>i</sub>∙ - 𝑋̅<sub>j</sub>∙) ± 𝑄<sub>α,I,I(J-1)</sub>√𝑀𝑆𝐸/𝐽

for every 𝑖 and 𝑗 (𝑖 = 1, ..., 𝐼 and 𝑗 = 1, ..., 𝐼) with 𝑖 < 𝑗, where 𝑄<sub>α,𝑚,𝑛</sub> denotes the upper-tail α critical value of the Studentized range distribution with m numerator df and v denominator df (analogous to 𝐹<sub>α,𝑣1,𝑣2</sub>).


General procedures for pairwise differences in ANOVA

List the sample means in increasing (decreasing) order and underline those pairs whose difference is less than the Bonferroni's or Tukey's width. Any pair of sample means not underscored by the same line corresponds to a pair of population means that are significantly different. This is called a lines plot.


Example 26.7 An experiment was carried out to compare five different brands of automobile oil filters with respect to their ability to capture foreign material.

Let µ<sub>i</sub> denote the true average amount of material captured by brand 𝑖 filters (𝑖 = 1, ..., 5) under controlled conditions. A sample of nine filters of each brand was used, resulting in the following sample mean amounts: 𝑥̅<sub>1</sub> = 14.5, 𝑥̅<sub>2</sub> = 13.8, 𝑥̅<sub>3</sub> = 13.3, 𝑥̅<sub>4</sub> = 14.3, and 𝑥̅<sub>5</sub> = 13.1.

Stage 1: The ANOVA table summarizes the analysis:

| Source of Variation | df | Sum of Squares | Mean Square | 𝑓 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Treatments (brands) | 4 | 13.32 | 3.33 | 37.84 |
| Error | 40 | 16.85 | 0.088 |  |
| Total | 44 |  |  |  |

Since 𝐹<sub>0.95,4,40</sub> = 2.61, 𝐻<sub>0</sub> is rejected (decisively) at level 0.05.

Stage 2: We now use Tukey's procedure to look for significant differences among the µ<sub>i</sub>’s.

From Appendix Table A.10, 𝑄<sub>0.05,5,40</sub> = 4.04 (the second subscript on Q table is 𝐼 and not 𝐼 – 1 as in F table), so the Tukey's width 𝑤 = 4.04√0.088/9 = 0.4.
