**Comparing means of 3 or more groups (ANOVA)**

In the previous chapter, we carry out the comparison between the means/proportions of two population means. Now we further extend the study to a comparison of more than two population or treatment means.

**Single Factor ANOVA Cont.**

**Example 23.1 revisit**  µ<sub>i</sub> denoting the true average compression strength for boxes of type i (i = 1, 2, 3, 4), the null hypothesis is

H<sub>0</sub>:µ<sub>1</sub> = µ<sub>2</sub> = µ<sub>3</sub> = µ<sub>4</sub>

| Type of Box | Compression Strength (lb) | Sample Mean | Sample SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 655.5 788.3 734.3 721.4 679.1 699.4 | 713.00 | 46.55 |
| 2 | 789.2 772.5 786.9 686.1 732.1 774.8 | 756.93 | 40.34 |
| 3 | 737.1 639.0 696.3 671.7 717.2 727.1 | 698.07 | 37.20 |
| 4 | 535.1 628.7 542.4 559.0 586.9 520.0 | 562.02 | 39.87 |

Grand mean = 682.50

The values of I and J for the strength data are 4 and 6, respectively, so numerator DFT<sub>r</sub> = I-1 = 3 and denominator DFE = N-I = I(J-1) = 20.

The grand mean is x̄<sub>..</sub> = Σ Σ x<sub>ij</sub>/(IJ) = 682.50,

MST<sub>r</sub> = [(713.00 – 682.50)<sup>2</sup> + (756.93 – 682.50)<sup>2</sup> + (698.07 – 682.50)<sup>2</sup> + (562.02 – 682.50)<sup>2</sup>] × 6/(4-1) = 42,455.86

MSE = [(6-1)46.55<sup>2</sup> + (5)40.34<sup>2</sup> + (5)37.20<sup>2</sup> + (5)39.87<sup>2</sup>]/(4 × 5) = 1691.92

f = MST<sub>r</sub>/MSE = 42,455.86/1691.92 = 25.09
