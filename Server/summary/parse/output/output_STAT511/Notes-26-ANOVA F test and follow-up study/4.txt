Example 26.3 (“anova_excel.xlsx”) A random sample of 15 healthy young men are split randomly into 3 groups of 5. They receive 0, 20, and 40 mg of the drug Paxil per day for a week. Then their serotonin levels are measured to determine whether Paxil affects serotonin levels.

Does Paxil affect serotonin levels in healthy young men?

Let μ<sub>1</sub> be the mean serotonin level for men receiving 0 mg of Paxil.
Let μ<sub>2</sub> be the mean serotonin level for men receiving 20 mg of Paxil.
Let μ<sub>3</sub> be the mean serotonin level for men receiving 40 mg of Paxil.

H<sub>0</sub>: μ<sub>1</sub> = μ<sub>2</sub> = μ<sub>3</sub>; mean serotonin levels are the same at all 3 dosage levels [or, mean serotonin levels are unaffected by Paxil dose]
H<sub>a</sub>: The mean serotonin levels of the three groups are not all equal [or, serotonin levels are affected by Paxil dose]

Use an overall F-Test: 𝑓<sub>𝑇𝑆</sub> = 𝑀𝑆𝐵/𝑀𝑆𝑊 has an F distribution with df: 𝑑𝑓𝑏 = 2, 𝑑𝑓𝑤 = 12 under H<sub>0</sub>.
