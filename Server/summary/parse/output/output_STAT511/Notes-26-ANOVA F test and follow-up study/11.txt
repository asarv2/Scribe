**Statistical Methods**

1. After arranging the five sample means in increasing order,

   13.1  13.3  13.8  14.3  14.5

the two smallest can be connected by a line segment because they differ by less than 0.4.

2. However, this segment cannot be extended further to the right since 13.8 – 13.1 = 0.7 ≥ 0.4.

3. Moving one mean to the right, the pair 𝑥̅<sub>3</sub> and 𝑥̅<sub>2</sub> cannot be underscored because these means differ by more than 0.4.

4. Again moving to the right, the next mean, 13.8, cannot be connected to any further to the right.

5. The last two means can be underscored with the same line segment.

Thus,

* Brands 1 and 4 are not significantly different from one another, but are significantly higher than the other three brands in their true average contents.

* Brand 2 is significantly better than 3 and 5 but worse than 1 and 4.

* Brands 3 and 5 do not differ significantly.
