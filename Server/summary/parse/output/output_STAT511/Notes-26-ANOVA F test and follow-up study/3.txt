**Statistical Methods**

**SST = Σ Σ (x<sub>ij</sub> - x̄<sub>..</sub>)<sup>2</sup> = Σ Σ x<sub>ij</sub><sup>2</sup> - (1/N) x̄<sub>..</sub><sup>2</sup>**

**SSTr = Σ Σ (x̄<sub>i</sub>. - x̄<sub>..</sub>)<sup>2</sup> = Σ (1/n<sub>i</sub>) x̄<sub>i</sub>.<sup>2</sup> - (1/N) x̄<sub>..</sub><sup>2</sup>**

**SSE = Σ Σ (x<sub>ij</sub> - x̄<sub>i</sub>.)<sup>2</sup> = Σ (n<sub>i</sub> - 1) s<sub>i</sub><sup>2</sup>**

**SST = SSTr + SSE**

**MSTr = SSTr / DFT<sub>r</sub>**

**MSE = SSE / DFE**

**F = MSTr / MSE**

**Example 26.2** Data on elastic modulus (GPa) obtained by a new ultrasonic method for specimens of a certain alloy produced using three different casting processes.

**Let μ<sub>1</sub>, μ<sub>2</sub>, μ<sub>3</sub>** denote the true average elastic moduli for the three different processes under the given circumstances.

1. **The relevant hypotheses are**

   H<sub>0</sub>: μ<sub>1</sub> = μ<sub>2</sub> = μ<sub>3</sub>
   versus
   H<sub>a</sub>: at least two of the μ<sub>i</sub>'s are different.

2. **The grand mean x̄<sub>..</sub> is 983.7/22 = 44.71.**

   The variances are 0.39, 0.25, 0.30 for each group.

   SSE = 0.39 × (8 - 1) + 0.25 × (8 - 1) + 0.30 × (6 - 1) = 6.00

   SSTr = (44.71 - 44.71)<sup>2</sup> × 8 + (44.06 - 44.71)<sup>2</sup> × 8 + (45.58 - 44.71)<sup>2</sup> × 6 = 7.93
