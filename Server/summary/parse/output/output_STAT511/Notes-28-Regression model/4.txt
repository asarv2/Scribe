The divisor 𝑛 – 2 in 𝑠<sup>2</sup> is the number of degrees of freedom (df) associated with SSE and the estimate 𝑠<sup>2</sup>. This is because to obtain 𝑠<sup>2</sup>, the two parameters 𝛽0 and 𝛽1 must first be estimated, which results in a loss of 2 df (just as 𝜇 had to be estimated in one sample problems, resulting in an estimated variance based on 𝑛−1 df).

An interpretation of 𝜎̂ is the size of a typical vertical deviation within the sample from the estimated regression line.

Note: The three estimators 𝑏0, 𝑏1 and 𝜎̂<sup>2</sup> are all UNBIASED estimators.
