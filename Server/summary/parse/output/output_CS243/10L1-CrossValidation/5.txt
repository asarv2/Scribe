The center robot represents the practice exam phase, where the model is tested on a set of examples similar to the training data. The right robot represents the final exam phase, where the model is tested on a set of examples that are completely new and different from the training data. The different expressions of the robots are meant to visually represent the different stages of the process and the increasing difficulty of the testing phase. The slide is likely to be followed by a discussion of the importance of testing models on unseen data to evaluate their generalization ability.  The diagrams on the whiteboard are simple representations of data used to train the model. The different exams represent different levels of testing.
