# 17

**Conversion to CNF**

B ⇔ (P ∨ Q)

Eliminate ⇔, replacing α ⇔ β with (α ⇒ β)∧(β ⇒ α)

• (B ⇒ (P ∨ Q)) ∧ ((P ∨ Q) ⇒ B)

Eliminate ⇒, replacing α ⇒ β with ¬α∨ β.

• (¬B ∨ P ∨ Q) ∧ (¬(P ∨ Q) ∨ B)

Move ¬ inwards using de Morgan's rules and double-negation:

• (¬B ∨ P ∨ Q) ∧ ((¬P ∧ ¬ Q) ∨ B)

Apply distributivity law (∧ over ∨) and flatten:

• (¬B ∨ P ∨ Q) ∧ (¬P ∨ B) ∧ (¬ Q ∨ B)
