**Description of what you see:**

This slide, part of a lecture on K-means clustering, illustrates the computation of centroids in the K-means algorithm.  The scatter plot shows a dataset with three distinct clusters.  Each cluster is visually separated by color (red, dark gray, and light blue).  The star-shaped markers represent the calculated centroids for each cluster.  The centroids are positioned near the center of mass of the data points within each cluster.  The purpose of this slide is to demonstrate how the algorithm calculates the centroids after the initial random assignment of data points to clusters.  The slide visually shows the result of the relocation of means step in the K-means algorithm.  The title "Example: Compute Centroids" clearly indicates the purpose of the plot.  The plot shows the data points and the calculated centroids, which are the means of the data points in each cluster.
