**How to choose k in kmeans?**

How many clusters do we need?
K-means optimization
* Minimize the sum of squared distances from each point to its cluster centroid
(this is called the inertia score in sklearn)
Naive approach to choose k:
* Try different values of k, pick one that minimizes inertia
* What is wrong with this? As k increases the inertia
score cannot increase, thus will pick k=n
Better approach:
* Pick k at the "elbow" of the plot
* Balances accuracy (low value of inertia) and complexity (small value of k)
