**Description of what you see:**

This slide, part of a lecture on K-means clustering, illustrates the reassignment step in the K-means algorithm.  The scatter plot shows a dataset with three distinct clusters, more clearly defined than in the previous example.  Each cluster is visually separated by color (red, dark gray, and light blue).  The data points within each cluster are now closer to each other, indicating that the algorithm has iteratively refined the cluster assignments.  The star-shaped markers represent the centroids of the clusters, which have likely shifted from their initial positions in the previous example.  The purpose of this slide is to demonstrate the iterative nature of the K-means algorithm, where data points are reassigned to the nearest centroid in each iteration until convergence.  The title "Example: Reassign Clusters" clearly indicates the purpose of the plot.  The plot shows the data points and the updated centroids, which are the means of the data points in each cluster after the reassignment step.  The reassignment of points to clusters is based on the distance to the centroids.  The colors and positions of the points now reflect a more refined clustering compared to the previous random assignment.
