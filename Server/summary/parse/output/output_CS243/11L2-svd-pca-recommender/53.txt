The plot is positioned centrally on the slide.  The purpose of the plot is to visually illustrate the bias-variance tradeoff in model complexity. The legend ("Plain" and "With biases") helps to distinguish between the two models.  The two lines on the graph visually demonstrate the trade-off between model complexity and prediction accuracy. The plot is a key visual aid for understanding the concept of bias-variance tradeoff in the context of recommender systems.
