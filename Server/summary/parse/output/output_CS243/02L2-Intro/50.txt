**AlphaGo System**

**Deep learning + Search**

One model predicts next move, given the current state of the board, trained on 30 million positions from human games.

Another model predicts the likelihood of winning given the current state, trained on 30 million positions from self-play.

System combines two models using Monte Carlo search.

Silver, David, et al. "Mastering the game of Go with deep neural networks and tree search." *Nature* 529.7587 (2016): 484-489.
