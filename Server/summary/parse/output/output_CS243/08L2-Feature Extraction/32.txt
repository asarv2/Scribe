# Slide 32

**Bag of features approach for object recognition**

Fed into classifiers (Logistic regression, Perceptron, etc)

**(Figure):**

The slide displays a combination of a bar graph and a set of small grayscale images.

* **Bar Graph:** A bar graph is positioned on the left side of the slide. The x-axis is labeled "codewords," and the y-axis is labeled "frequency."  The bars are various shades of pink, and their heights represent the frequency of occurrence of different codewords.


* **Small Grayscale Images:** A set of small, grayscale images are arranged horizontally below the bar graph.  These images represent the "codewords."  The images are various shades of gray, and they appear to be different visual patterns or features.  One of these images is highlighted with a green box, and another is highlighted with a red box.  The highlighted images are likely examples of the most frequent or important codewords.


* **Image with Bounding Boxes:** A grayscale image of a street scene with several cars is shown in the upper right quadrant of the slide.  Several bounding boxes (red and green squares) are drawn around the cars in the image.  This image is used to illustrate the application of the Bag of Features approach to object recognition.


**Description of what you see:**

This slide introduces the "Bag of Features" approach to object recognition.  The bar graph shows the frequency of different visual features (codewords) extracted from an image.  The small grayscale images represent these codewords.  The image with bounding boxes demonstrates how the approach is used to identify objects (cars) in an image.  The green and red boxes highlight specific codewords, likely indicating important features for object recognition.  The slide is designed to visually explain the core idea of Bag of Features, a technique used in computer vision for object recognition.  The approach involves extracting visual features from an image, representing them as codewords, and then using these codewords to classify objects.  The classifiers (e.g., logistic regression, perceptron) are trained on these codeword frequencies to recognize objects.
