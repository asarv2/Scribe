**Derivatives and convolution**

Differentiation is linear

∂(af(x) + bg(x))
∂x
= a ∂f(x)
∂x
+ b ∂g(x)
∂x

Differentiation is shift-invariant: Derivative of shifted signal is shifted derivative

Hence, differentiation can be represented as convolution!
