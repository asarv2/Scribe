**Context:**

This slide, part of a lecture on AI Basics, continues the discussion of challenges in image classification.  It introduces the challenge of occlusion, where parts of an object are hidden or obscured by other objects or elements in the image. The images are presented to visually demonstrate how occlusion can make it difficult for a computer vision system to accurately identify and classify objects.  The images are examples of how parts of the cat are hidden by other objects, making it harder to recognize the cat.  The slide builds on previous slides that discussed viewpoint variation, illumination, and deformation as challenges in image classification.

The images are of cats, but in each case, parts of the cats are hidden by other objects (a blanket, grass, or a couch).
