**Context:**

This slide, part of a lecture on AI Basics, continues the discussion of challenges in image classification.  It introduces the challenge of background clutter, where distracting elements in the background can make it difficult for a computer vision system to focus on the object of interest (in this case, the cat). The images are presented to visually demonstrate how a cluttered background can make it harder to recognize the cat.  The slide builds on previous slides that discussed viewpoint variation, illumination, deformation, and occlusion as challenges in image classification.

The images are of cats, but in each case, the background is visually busy, with leaves, twigs, and snow.  The text below each image indicates the licensing of the image, which is important for understanding the rights associated with using the image.  The overall design of the slide is simple and straightforward, focusing on the visual representation of the concept of background clutter.
