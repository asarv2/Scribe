**Context:**

This slide, part of a lecture on AI Basics, follows the previous slides that discussed challenges in image classification, such as viewpoint variation and illumination.  The current slide introduces the challenge of deformation in image classification.  The images demonstrate how different poses and positions of the same object (in this case, cats) can make it difficult for a computer vision system to reliably identify the object.  The images are used as visual aids to illustrate the concept of deformation and its impact on image recognition.  The slide is designed to highlight the complexity of image recognition tasks and the need for robust algorithms that can handle variations in object appearance.
