# Slide 36

**Common types of noise**

Salt and pepper noise: random occurrences of black and white pixels
Impulse noise: random occurrences of white pixels
Gaussian noise: variations in intensity drawn from a Gaussian normal distribution

**(Image Description):**

The slide presents a 2x2 grid of images.  Each image in the grid is a grayscale portrait of a person wearing a hat.

* **Top Left:**  The image is labeled "Original."  It shows a clear, unadulterated grayscale portrait.

* **Top Right:** The image is labeled "Salt and pepper noise."  It shows the same portrait, but with small, randomly placed black and white pixels superimposed on it.  These black and white pixels are the noise.

* **Bottom Left:** The image is labeled "Impulse noise."  It shows the same portrait, but with small, randomly placed white pixels superimposed on it.  These white pixels are the noise.

* **Bottom Right:** The image is labeled "Gaussian noise."  It shows the same portrait, but with a speckled, grainy appearance.  The noise appears as variations in the intensity of the pixels, creating a grainy effect.  This is different from the salt-and-pepper noise, which is more discrete.
