**Description of the slide:**

This slide, part of a lecture on AI Basics, presents a bar graph illustrating the improvement in ImageNet Top-5 classification accuracy over time.  The x-axis represents the years (2010-2017), and the y-axis represents the classification error rate.  Each bar represents the classification error for a specific year.  The bars are colored differently, making it easy to visually compare the error rates across the years.  The graph shows a clear downward trend in the classification error rate, indicating that image recognition models are becoming more accurate over time.  The text "AlexNet, The Beginning" highlights a significant milestone in this improvement, specifically mentioning that AlexNet was the first GPU-accelerated deep convolutional neural network to win an image recognition contest.  The percentages (16.7% and 23.3%) and the downward arrows indicate the percentage decrease in error rate from the previous year.  The source of the data is also cited at the bottom of the slide.  The overall purpose of the slide is to demonstrate the progress and evolution of image recognition technology, with a focus on the impact of AlexNet.
