**Searching States – Chess is a classic example**

When game-playing AI plays chess; it creates and models as many states as it can.

* At a basic level – what does the board look like if that piece is moved?
* Evaluate if it’s good or bad
* Create and consider as many as possible

**Some Observations:**

* The search tree is large: White has 20 possible first moves, as does black. 400 states in 2 moves.
* Black and White are searching for different outcomes
* For most computers, searching the entire space isn’t feasible

Before we get further into game playing, lets consider some simpler search spaces.
