# Slide 20

**Example: The Compass System**

Prediction Fails Differently for Black Defendants

|  | WHITE | AFRICAN AMERICAN |
|---|---|---|
| Labeled Higher Risk, But Didn't Re-Offend | 23.5% | 44.9% |
| Labeled Lower Risk, Yet Did Re-Offend | 47.7% | 28.0% |


Non-offending blacks get higher scores

* NB: race is not a feature in the model

So the recall for the "positive" case isn't equal for the groups

Source: Richard Johansson, University of Gothenburg

The percentages for Black defendants are significantly higher in the "Higher Risk" category and lower in the "Lower Risk" category compared to White defendants.  The note "NB: race is not a feature in the model" highlights that the model does not explicitly use race as an input, yet the outcome shows a disparity.  The conclusion, "So the recall for the 'positive' case isn't equal for the groups," emphasizes the unequal accuracy of the model's predictions for different racial groups.  The source is clearly attributed.
