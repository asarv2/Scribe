# Slide 12

**Bias in Data Models**

"Objective" algorithms are thought to be free of the biases that plague people.
Algorithms, especially ones that learn, can inadvertently re-inscribe those biases
Algorithms are opaque, hard to interrogate
Increasingly widespread

Source: Lawrence Hunter, Colorado School of Mines

This slide, part of the AI Basics lecture, discusses the inherent biases that can be present in data models, despite the common assumption that algorithms are objective. The final point emphasizes the increasing prevalence of algorithms in various applications.  The source is clearly attributed, which is a standard practice in academic presentations.
