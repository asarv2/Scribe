**Resolution Rule**

If a formula φ contains clauses (x ∨ α) and (¬x ∨ β),
then one can derive a new clause (α ∨ β)

**Description:**

This slide presents the Resolution Rule, a fundamental inference rule in propositional logic, used in automated theorem proving, including SAT solvers.  The rule states that if a formula contains two clauses, one containing a literal (x) and the other containing the negation of that literal (¬x), then a new clause can be derived by combining the remaining literals (α and β) from the two clauses using the logical OR operator (∨).  The slide is a concise statement of the resolution rule, which is a key concept in logical reasoning and automated deduction.  There are no figures on this slide.
