**Perceptron loss:**

l(z) = max{0, -z}

**Hinge loss:**

l(z) = max{0, 1 - z}

**Logistic loss:**

l(z) = log(1 + e<sup>-z</sup>)

**Reminder:**

z = y(w<sup>T</sup>x)
