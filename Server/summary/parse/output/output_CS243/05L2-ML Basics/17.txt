Let's predict CO2 from Volume and Weight:

y<sub>i</sub>(CO<sub>2</sub>) = β<sub>0</sub> + β<sub>1</sub>V<sub>i</sub> + β<sub>2</sub>W<sub>i</sub>

Can be written as:

y<sub>i</sub> = (1  V<sub>i</sub>  W<sub>i</sub>)  $\begin{pmatrix} β_0 \\ β_1 \\ β_2 \end{pmatrix}$

Hence, the four data points can be written as:

$\begin{pmatrix} y_1 \\ \vdots \\ y_4 \end{pmatrix}$ = $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & V_1 & W_1 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ 1 & V_4 & W_4 \end{pmatrix}$ $\begin{pmatrix} β_0 \\ β_1 \\ β_2 \end{pmatrix}$
