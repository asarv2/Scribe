**Solving Equations**

With invertible matrices, we can solve the system of equations as such:

Ax = b

A<sup>-1</sup>Ax = A<sup>-1</sup>b

Since A<sup>-1</sup>A is the identity matrix, A<sup>-1</sup>Ax = Ix = x = A<sup>-1</sup>b
