# Tuple Ambiguity in Join

We want to join our Invoice table with our Customer Table, but the tuples produce ambiguity
Invoice (InvoiceId, CustomerId,...)
Customer (CustomerId,...)
If we try to join, we have to deal with this ambiguity
SELECT CustomerId, FirstName, LastName, BillingCity, BillingState, Total
FROM Invoice
JOIN Customer
ON CustomerId = CustomerId
WHERE BillingCountry="USA" AND Total > 15
