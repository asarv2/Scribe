**Having Clause**

The `HAVING` clause allows you to apply conditions on aggregates.

```sql
SELECT BillingCountry, SUM(Total) AS Total, COUNT(*) AS Invoices
FROM Invoice
WHERE Total > 10
GROUP BY BillingCountry
HAVING Invoices > 5
```

This SQL query calculates the sum of `Total` and the count of `Invoices` for each `BillingCountry` where the `Total` is greater than 10.  The results are grouped by `BillingCountry`, and then filtered further by the `HAVING` clause, which ensures that only countries with more than 5 invoices are included in the output.  The output table shows the `BillingCountry`, the sum of `Total` for invoices in that country, and the count of invoices for that country.


| BillingCountry | Total | Invoices |
|---|---|---|
| Canada | 110.88 | 8 |
| USA | 220.03 | 15 |
