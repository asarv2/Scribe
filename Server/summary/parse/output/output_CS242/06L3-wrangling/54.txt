**Simple grouping**

Look at our stock dataset

```
df_stock = pd.read_csv('stocks.csv')
df_stock = pd.read_csv('stocks.csv', parse_dates=['Date'])
```

Use `groupby` to split your DataFrame systematically

```
grouped_stock = df_stock.groupby('Symbol')
for stock, stock_df in grouped_stock:
    print(stock)
    display(stock_df)
```

Or pull a specific group as needed:

```
grouped_stock.get_group('GOOG')
```
