# Slide 36

**Abstracting map+reduce**

To use this framework, the programmer only needs to implement the mapper and the 
reducer function—the execution engine can use whatever method it wants to actually 
compute the result 
The application of the mapper functions is inherently parallel and can be carried out in 
separate threads/machines 
In many cases, the reduce step can also be carried out incrementally


**Description:**

This slide discusses the abstraction level of the MapReduce framework.  It emphasizes that the programmer only needs to define the `mapper` and `reducer` functions.  The framework's execution engine handles the details of distributing the work and combining the results.  The slide also highlights the inherent parallelism of the mapper functions and the potential for incremental reduction in the reducer function.  The slide is entirely text-based, with no figures or images.  The content is focused on the conceptual description of the MapReduce abstraction, emphasizing the programmer's role and the framework's role in handling the distributed computation.
