**Description of the slide:**

This slide, part of a lecture on Intro to Data Science, focuses on the "Volume" aspect of big data.  It uses a visual representation to illustrate the massive scale of data creation and storage.

* **Visual Elements:**
    * **2020 and 2005:**  Two stylized computer memory chip icons, one labeled 2020 and the other 2005, are positioned above the text about the increase in data.  They are meant to represent the time period and the growth of data storage capacity.
    * **Globe:** A stylized, 3D globe, colored orange and blue, is positioned in the lower left quadrant.  The text "WORLD POPULATION: 7 BILLION" is below the globe.  This visual element is used to contextualize the sheer number of people generating data.
    * **Smartphone:** A stylized smartphone icon is positioned above the globe.  The text "6 BILLION PEOPLE have cell phones" is next to it.  This icon and text highlight the widespread use of cell phones and the resulting data generation.
    * **Data Bars:**  A series of stacked, 3D data bars are positioned in the middle of the slide.  These bars are colored and represent the growth of data over time.  The bars are meant to visually represent the increasing volume of data.
    * **Data Cloud:** A stylized cloud of data blocks (different colors) is positioned in the upper right quadrant.  This visual element is meant to represent the sheer volume of data being created.
    * **Building:** A stylized building is positioned in the lower middle quadrant.  The text "Most companies in the U.S. have at least 100 TERABYTES [100,000 GIGABYTES] of data stored" is next to it.  This visual element is used to illustrate the amount of data stored by companies.

* **Purpose:** The slide aims to convey the massive increase in data creation and storage over time, highlighting the volume aspect of big data.  The various visual elements are used to make the information more engaging and easier to understand.  The use of different colors and 3D shapes adds visual interest and helps to distinguish the different data points.
