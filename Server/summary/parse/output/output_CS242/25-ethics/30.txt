**Intelligible Models for HealthCare**

Caruana et al. 2015

In the mid 90's, Cost-Effective HealthCare (CEHC) funded a large study to evaluate the application of machine learning to predicting pneumonia risk.

**Goal:** predict the probability of death (POD) for patients with pneumonia so high-risk patients could be admitted to the hospital while low-risk patients treated as outpatients.

Most accurate models were neural nets, which outperformed traditional methods by wide margin.

But after careful consideration they were considered too risky for use on real patients and logistic regression was used instead. Why?
