**Can Predictive Systems Discriminate Unfairly**

When predictive systems are more widely applied in situations where they affect people's lives, they may run into troubles concerning anti-discrimination laws.
There is no consensus about how to define and even less how to deal with the problem.

Source: Richard Johansson, University of Gothenburg
