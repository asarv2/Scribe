# Slide 44

**The competition**

Netflix had ~2000 Employees, less in engineering, less on recommender
Competition was open to anyone
* Data was anonymized
* Competitors submit predictions for 3 million test items
* At most 1 submission per day
Prize of $1,000,000 if error is 10% lower than Netflix's current system
* Improvements of 1% from the previous year qualifies for a smaller $50k prize
Slide via: Padhraic Smyth, UC Irvine CS277


**Description:**

This slide describes the Netflix Prize competition.  The text details the competition's characteristics:

* **Participants:** Netflix had approximately 2000 employees, with fewer involved in engineering and recommender systems. The competition was open to anyone.

* **Data:** The data used in the competition was anonymized.

* **Submissions:** Competitors submitted predictions for 3 million test items.  There was a limit of one submission per day.

* **Prizes:** A $1,000,000 prize was awarded if the error rate was 10% lower than Netflix's current system.  Improvements of 1% from the previous year also qualified for a smaller $50,000 prize.
