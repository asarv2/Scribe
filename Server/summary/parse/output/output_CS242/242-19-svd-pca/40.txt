# Slide 40

**Modeling Systematic Biases**

```
rij ≈ μ + b<sub>i</sub> + b<sub>j</sub> + u<sub>i</sub> ⋅ v<sub>j</sub>
```

Rating: Mean rating + bias for user i + bias for movie j + user-movie interactions

**Example:**

* Mean rating μ = 3.7
* You are a critical reviewer; your ratings are 1 lower than the mean: b<sub>i</sub> = −1
* Star Wars gets a mean rating of 0.5 higher than average movie: b<sub>j</sub> = 0.5
* Predicted rating for you on Star Wars: 3.7 + 1 − 0.5 = 3.2
