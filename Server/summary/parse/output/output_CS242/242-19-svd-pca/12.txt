# Slide 12

**SVD for matrix reconstruction**

Columns of U: latent factor affinities for entities (n)
Rows of V<sup>T</sup>: latent factor affinities for attributes (d)
Diagonal entries of S: eigenvalues of A
Latent factors reconstruct matrix A
A[i, j] = U[i, :] S V<sup>T</sup>[:, j]
Eigenvalues in S only scale the value in U or V
Let V<sup>T</sup> = S V<sup>T</sup>
A[i, j] = U[i, :] V<sup>T</sup>[:, j]
Dot product of the Entity's latent factor with the Attribute's latent factor to get the Entity i's jth attribute value
Use only the top k factors to approximate results
