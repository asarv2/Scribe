**Central limit theorem**

The central limit theorem states that the sample mean  (for "reasonably sized" samples, e.g., m > 30) has a Gaussian distribution regardless of the distribution of X.

  
In practice, for m < 30 and when we estimate  using the sample variance, we should use a Student's t-distribution with m-1 degrees of freedom.
