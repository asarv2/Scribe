# Slide 19

**Example test: Student's T-Test**

The t-test was introduced in 1908 by William Gosset
Used to monitor and ensure quality at the Guinness Brewery
"Student's" was to disguise the fact that they were using statistics in their methods
The test involved calculating the value of "t"
For larger sample sizes (i.e., m > 30) the t-distribution converges to normal

**Description of what you see:**

This slide introduces the Student's t-test, a statistical method.  It explains that the t-test was developed in 1908 by William Gosset, who used it to monitor quality at the Guinness Brewery.  The pseudonym "Student" was used to disguise the fact that statistical methods were being employed.  The t-test involves calculating a value "t".  The slide also notes that for larger sample sizes (greater than 30), the t-distribution approaches a normal distribution.  The slide is part of a lecture on statistical methods, specifically introducing the t-test.  There are no figures on this slide.
