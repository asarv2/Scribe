# Slide 31

**Why not always use 1-sided tests?**

So … why is that the case? Why not always use one-sided t-tests for smaller p-values?
* A p value of .05, indicates that if you look at something 100 times, you’d make the wrong conclusion 5 times.
* A 2-tailed test is looking at 2 things: Greater than? Less than?
* A 1-tailed test is only making 1 observation, but you have to make that choice before looking at the data.
