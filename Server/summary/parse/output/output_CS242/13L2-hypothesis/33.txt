# Slide 33

**Two sample test**

The previous example assumed that the null hypothesis corresponds to a known mean, but in reality we may not know the mean that we want to compare to.  In this case, we often formulate hypothesis about difference in means

H<sub>0</sub> : μ<sub>0</sub> = μ<sub>1</sub>(or e.g. μ<sub>0</sub> ≥ μ<sub>1</sub>)
H<sub>1</sub> : μ<sub>0</sub> ≠ μ<sub>1</sub>(or e.g. μ<sub>0</sub> < μ<sub>1</sub>)

In this case, we can use a two-sample t-test
