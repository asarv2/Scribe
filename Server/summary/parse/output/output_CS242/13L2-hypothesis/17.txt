**Description of what you see:**

This slide continues the example from the previous slide, focusing on the sample average as a random variable.  It provides the mathematical formulas for calculating the expected value (mean) and variance of the sample average.  The formulas show how the mean of the sample average is equal to the population mean, and the variance of the sample average is equal to the population variance divided by the sample size.  The key takeaway is that the sample average is a random variable with its own distribution, and this distribution is centered around the population mean and has a variance inversely proportional to the sample size.  The figure visually reinforces this concept by showing how different samples from the same population can result in different sample averages.  The figure is a key component of the slide, illustrating the variability of the sample average.
