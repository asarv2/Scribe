# Slide 22

**Logic of Hypothesis testing**

Effort is made to reject the null hypothesis
If H₀ is rejected, we tentatively conclude H₁ to be the case
Otherwise we fail to reject the null (we can never prove the null to be true)
Role of evidence: Null is assumed true and a contradiction is sought in order to reject it.

**Description of what you see:**

This slide, part of a lecture on Intro to Data Science, explains the logic behind hypothesis testing.  The text emphasizes that the goal is to *reject* the null hypothesis (H₀), rather than prove it true.  If the null hypothesis is rejected, then the alternative hypothesis (H₁) is tentatively accepted.  Crucially, the text states that failing to reject the null hypothesis does not mean the null hypothesis is proven true; it simply means there wasn't enough evidence to reject it.
