**Basic Approach to hypothesis testing**

Posit a null hypothesis  and an alternative hypothesis  (usually just that “
 is not true”),

*  : No difference in average user engagement (time spent on site) between design A and design B.
*  : With new design (B) users spend more time on site, on average

Collect data  that will be used to test hypothesis, e.g.,

* Measure user times under both design A (blue) and B (red)
* Average times: A=30.65s, B=37.7s
