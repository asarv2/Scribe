# Example: Dog Breeds

A circular dendrogram is displayed, showing the evolutionary relationships between various dog breeds.  The dendrogram is composed of a central circle, from which numerous branches radiate outward.  Each branch represents a dog breed, and the position of the branch on the circle and its connection to other branches indicates the evolutionary relationship.  The branches are colored differently, with different shades of colors representing different groups of dog breeds.  The dog breeds are labeled along the branches, with the names of the breeds written next to the corresponding branches.  The branches that are closer together on the circle represent breeds that are more closely related evolutionarily.  The dendrogram also includes a vertical dendrogram on the right side of the slide.  This vertical dendrogram shows the hierarchical clustering of the dog breeds based on their genetic similarity.  The vertical dendrogram is a separate, but related, representation of the same data.  The vertical dendrogram is a bar chart-like structure, where each bar represents a dog breed, and the height of the bar indicates the similarity to other breeds.  The vertical dendrogram is colored in shades of red, indicating the degree of similarity.  The dendrogram is positioned in the center of the slide, with the vertical dendrogram on the right side.  The legend at the bottom of the slide shows the color-coding for the similarity scores of the dog breeds.  The legend shows three different symbols: a gold circle, a black star, and a white star.  The gold circle represents a similarity score of 90-100, the black star represents a similarity score of 70-89, and the white star represents a similarity score of 50-69.
