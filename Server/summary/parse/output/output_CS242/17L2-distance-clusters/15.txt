# Slide 15

**Clustering terminology**

Hierarchical clustering
* clusters form a hierarchy
* computed bottom-up or top-down fashion

Flat clustering
* no inter-cluster structure

Hard clustering
* items are assigned to a single unique cluster

Soft clustering
* items are assigned to one or more clusters
* membership often expressed as a real-valued function

Kmeans is flat, hard clustering approach

**Description:**

This slide defines different types of clustering methods.  It's a table-like presentation, with clear headings for each type of clustering.  The slide is likely part of a lecture on clustering algorithms, introducing the various ways data points can be grouped.

* **Hierarchical Clustering:**  This method creates a hierarchy of clusters, often computed in a bottom-up or top-down manner.

* **Flat Clustering:**  This method doesn't have an inter-cluster structure, meaning clusters are independent of each other.

* **Hard Clustering:**  Data points are assigned to only one cluster.

* **Soft Clustering:**  Data points can be assigned to multiple clusters, with membership expressed as a real-valued function (e.g., a probability of belonging to each cluster).

* **K-means:**  A specific example of a flat, hard clustering approach.
