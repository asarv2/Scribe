# Slide 11

**Personas**

To provide a common understanding across all teams.

---

**Description:**

This slide displays twelve simple, cartoon-like portraits of people.  The figures are diverse in terms of gender, ethnicity, and apparent age.  Each portrait is a basic outline drawing, with simple features like hair, eyes, and mouth.  The figures are arranged in a grid pattern, two rows of six, across the slide.  The title "Personas" is displayed above the grid of portraits.  Below the grid, a short text description, "To provide a common understanding across all teams," is written.

**Reasoning:**

The purpose of this slide is to introduce the concept of personas in the context of data science.  Personas are representations of the target users of a product or service.  By creating these representations, teams can better understand the needs, motivations, and behaviors of their users.  The diverse range of portraits suggests that the lecture is emphasizing the importance of considering a variety of user types when designing data science solutions.  The text below the portraits further clarifies the purpose of using personas, which is to create a shared understanding across different teams working on the project.
