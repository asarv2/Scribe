**Quantitative evaluation**

For prediction tasks there are a variety of quantitative evaluation metrics
* Accuracy, squared loss, area under ROC, etc.
For cluster analysis there is typically no ground truth
* Evaluation aims measure the "goodness" of the resulting clusters
