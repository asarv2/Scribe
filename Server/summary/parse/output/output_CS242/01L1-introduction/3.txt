# Slide 3

**Goals**

Provide a broad introduction to the field of data science
Introduce the foundational computational and statistical tools for data science including:
* data acquisition, cleaning and manipulation
* analysis and evaluation
* visualization and communication of results
* data management and big data systems
Survey the complete data science process from data to knowledge and provide hands-on experience with tools and methods
