**Experiment 1: Due by the last day of class**

Any Lab/Homework can be turned in by the last day of class for full credit*

*   Deadlines still exist, but may be extended by the student's Lab GTA
*   A student may ask for at most 1 extension per week, in lab, in person

**Slide Description:**

This slide outlines the policy for turning in Experiment 1 lab/homework assignments.  The key takeaway is that any lab or homework can be submitted by the last day of class for full credit.  While deadlines exist, they can be extended under certain conditions.  Students can request one extension per week, in person during lab.  A one-week extension is automatically granted from the original due date.  Further extensions to the last day of class are possible if "Satisfactory" progress has been achieved.  Finally, the TAs may grant end-of-semester extensions for the week's lab if all students present have achieved "Satisfactory" status by the end of the session.  The slide is clear about the submission policy and the conditions under which extensions can be granted.
