# Slide 24

**Experiment 2: Less Granular Points**

There are 12 labs and maybe 6 Homeworks, they make up 20% of your grade. Individual points on these assignments don't matter. Students focus way to much on them.

On Labs/Homework:
* Above 90% is "Excellent" (100% for calculations in gradebook)
* 80-90% is "Good" (90% in gradebook)
* 70-80% is "Satisfactory" (80% in gradebook)
* 60-70% is "Progressing" (60% in gradebook)
* 0-60% is "Unsatisfactory" (0% in gradebook)

Projects will have a separate rubric, to be discussed later.
