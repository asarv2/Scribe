**Slide Description:**

This slide describes the Ed Discussion forum, which is a 24/7 online resource for asking questions about the Intro to Data Science course.  The forum allows students to ask questions anonymously, meaning their names are not visible to other students, but instructors can still see who posted the question.  Students are directed to Brightspace to access the link for the forum if they haven't already signed up.  The slide is designed to inform students about the Ed Discussion forum and how to access it.  There are no figures on this slide.
