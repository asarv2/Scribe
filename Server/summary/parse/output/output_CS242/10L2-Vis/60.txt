# Slide 60

**Choosing a Good Visualization**

*   **What are you trying to show?**  Consider the specific message or insight you want to communicate.  Are you looking to show a relationship, a distribution, a comparison, or the composition of something?

*   **What kind of data do you have?**  The type of data (categorical, numerical, time-series, etc.) will influence the best visualization method.

*   **What is the best way to show the data?**  Consider different visualization types (bar charts, scatter plots, histograms, line graphs, etc.) and how they can effectively represent the data and the message you want to convey.

*   **How can you make the graph easy to understand?**  Use clear labels, titles, and legends.  Ensure the graph is well-organized and visually appealing.  Consider the target audience and how they will interpret the information.
