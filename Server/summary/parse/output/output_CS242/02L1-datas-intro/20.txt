# Slide 20

**Personalized autism treatments**

*Kasari et al. 2014*

200 non-verbal children with autism at four institutions tested adaptive intervention strategies

* Those who started with iPad used ~21 words more than those who didn't

Uses sequential multiple assignment randomized trial (SMART): decision rules that specify how the intensity or type of treatment should change depending on the patient's needs

Reinforcement learning is used to estimate optimal sequences of decision rules.
