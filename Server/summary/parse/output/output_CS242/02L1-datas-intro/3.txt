# Slide 3

**Data Never Sleeps 4.0**

**Infographic**

A large infographic, presented by DOMO, displays data on various online activities and services.  The infographic is circular, with numerous smaller, circular icons representing different platforms (Buzzfeed, Snapchat, Netflix, Google, YouTube, Facebook Messenger, Amazon, Instagram, Siri, Tinder, Giphy, Twitter, Dropbox). Each icon has a corresponding statistic about the platform's activity per minute.

* **Buzzfeed:** 159,380 pieces of content viewed by Americans.
* **Snapchat:** 6,944,444 videos watched.
* **Netflix:** 86,805 hours of video streamed by subscribers.
* **Google:** 69,500,000 words translated.
* **YouTube:** 100 hours of new video shared.
* **Facebook Messenger:** 216,302 photos shared.
* **Amazon:** $222,283 in sales.
* **Instagram:** 2,430,555 posts liked.
* **Siri:** 99,206 requests answered.
* **Tinder:** 972,222 user swipes.
* **Giphy:** 569,217 GIFs served.
* **Twitter:** 9,678 emoji-filled tweets sent.
* **Dropbox:** 833,333 new files uploaded.
* **The Weather Channel:** 13,888,888 forecast requests received.

The infographic also includes a central clock icon representing "every minute of the day" in 2016.  The overall design is visually appealing, using different colors and icons to represent each platform.  A small box at the bottom right of the slide contains a text box that explains the infographic's data and the concept of "Data Never Sleeps."


**Description of the slide:**

This slide is a visually rich infographic, designed to showcase the massive amount of data generated every minute.  The slide uses a variety of colors and icons to represent different online platforms and services.  The data presented includes metrics like the number of videos watched, photos shared, and sales made, all within a single minute.  The infographic is intended to be engaging and informative, highlighting the sheer volume of data generated online.  The inclusion of the "Data Never Sleeps 4.0" title and the accompanying text box at the bottom right further emphasizes the theme of constant data generation.
