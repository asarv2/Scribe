**Description:**

This slide, likely part of a lecture on the history of data science, illustrates an early example of data analysis.  The image shows a plane with a concentration of red dots on the wings and fuselage.  These dots represent areas where the plane was most often hit during combat.  The image is intended to illustrate how data analysis was used to determine the most vulnerable areas of the plane, allowing for the strategic placement of armor to improve survivability.  The text below the image explains that during World War II, statistician Abraham Wald was asked to help the British determine where to add armor to their planes.
