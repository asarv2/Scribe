# Slide 4

**Noise**

Noise refers to measurement error in data values

* **Random errors:** inconsistencies in measurement, e.g., different people reporting heights measured with a ruler (some may be an overestimate others may be an underestimate)
* **Systematic (bias) errors:** methodological/personal errors that consistently deviate in one direction, e.g., heights measured with a "broken" ruler that is shorter than the numbers indicate
