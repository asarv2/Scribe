**Figure Description:**

The slide displays several clusters of small, densely packed black dots (representing data points) scattered across the slide.  These clusters are roughly spherical or elliptical in shape.  There are also several single, isolated black dots positioned outside of the main clusters.  These isolated dots are the outliers.  The placement of these clusters and outliers is meant to visually represent how outliers differ from the majority of data points in a dataset.  The presence of the clusters and outliers is intended to illustrate the concept of outliers in a data set. The orange circles are not part of the data, but are used to highlight the outliers.
