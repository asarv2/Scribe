## Slide 32

**p-values considered harmful**

p-values: probability of your data given the null hypothesis

Common Mistakes: probability of the null hypothesis given the data

* The p value cannot tell you your hypothesis is correct
* The p value does not tell you the probability that your result occurred just by random chance
* The p values cannot tell you the effect size or the result's importance

p < 0.05 is not a magic line separating real results from false ones
* It is one piece evidence to consider
