**Example in R**

```R
> library(MASS)
> contTable = table(survey$Smoke, survey$Exer)
> contTable
        Freq None Some
  Heavy    7    1    3
  Never   87   18   84
  Occas   12    3    4
  Regul    9    1    7
> chisq.test(contTable)

Pearson's Chi-squared test
data:  contTable
X-squared = 5.4885, df = 6, p-value = 0.4828
```

The `chisq.test()` function is then used to perform the chi-square test on the contingency table.  The output shows the chi-square statistic, degrees of freedom, and p-value.
