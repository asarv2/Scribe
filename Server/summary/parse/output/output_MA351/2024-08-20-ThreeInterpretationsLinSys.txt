\textbf{Solution:} $(x, y) = (3, 2)$, or $(x, y) = (3, 2)$

Solution of $(*)$ (using elimination \& substitution)

\begin{align*}
\begin{cases}
x + 2y + z = 1 \\
3x + y + 4z = 0 \\
2x + 2y + 3z = 2
\end{cases}
\end{align*}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $\implies$ $x = 1 - 2y - z$
    \item $\implies$ $3(1 - 2y - z) + y + 4z = 0 \implies -5y + z = -3$
    \item $\implies$ $2(1 - 2y - z) + 2y + 3z = 2 \implies -2y + z = 0$
\end{enumerate}
$y = 1$
$z = 2$
$x = 1 - 2(1) - 2 = -3$

Solution: $(x, y, z) = (-3, 1, 2)$
