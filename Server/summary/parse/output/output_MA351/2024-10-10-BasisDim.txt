\textbf{Basis and Dimension}

Given a vector space $V$, a basis is a list of vectors $B = \{v_1, v_2, ..., v_n\}$ that satisfies:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item (1) each $v_i \in V$
    \item (2) $B$ is linearly independent, i.e., $c_1v_1 + c_2v_2 + ... + c_n v_n = 0 \Rightarrow c_1 = c_2 = ... = c_n = 0$
    \item (3) $B$ spans/fills the whole $V$, i.e., for any $v \in V$, there are $c_1, c_2, ..., c_n$ such that $c_1v_1 + c_2v_2 + ... + c_n v_n = v$
\end{enumerate}

Slide 7

Properties of $dim(V)$

\begin{itemize}
    \item $Ptf$: Any $m$ vectors of $V$, $m > dim(V)$, must be linearly dependent.
    \item Hence $m \ge dim(V)$.
    \item Any list of $m$ linearly independent vectors in $V$, $m \le dim(V)$.
    \item $Ptf$: Any homogeneous system of $m$ linear equations with at least one more unknown than equations must have at least one free unknown.
    \item Hence $m \le n$
    \item $dim(V) = n$
\end{itemize}
