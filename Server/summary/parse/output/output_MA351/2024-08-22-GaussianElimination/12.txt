Examples: Express pivot var. in terms of free var.

(a)
\begin{align*}
\begin{cases}
x + y + z + w = 5 \\
x + y - z + w = 7 \\
x + y + 2z - w = 0
\end{cases}
\end{align*}

$\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & | & 5 \\
1 & 1 & -1 & 1 & | & 7 \\
1 & 1 & 2 & -1 & | & 0
\end{bmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & | & 5 \\
0 & 0 & -2 & 0 & | & 2 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & -2 & | & -5
\end{bmatrix}$

$(x, z, w \text{ are pivot}), \quad y \text{ is free}$
