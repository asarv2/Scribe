Examples: Express pivot var. in terms of free var.

(c)
\begin{align*}
x - y + z = 5
\end{align*}

$\begin{bmatrix}
1 & -1 & 1 & | & 5
\end{bmatrix}$

$z = t$ (free)
$y = s$ (free)
$x = y + z + 5 = s + t + 5$

Solution:
$\begin{bmatrix}
x \\
y \\
z
\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
s + t + 5 \\
s \\
t
\end{bmatrix}$
