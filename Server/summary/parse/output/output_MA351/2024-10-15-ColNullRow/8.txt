\textbf{Description:}

This slide discusses the relationships between the number of columns, rows, and the rank of a matrix $A$.  It introduces the concepts of linear independence for the columns and rows of a matrix.  The slide states that the columns of $A$ are linearly independent if and only if the rank of $A$ is equal to the number of columns (n).  Similarly, the rows of $A$ are linearly independent if and only if the rank of $A$ is equal to the number of rows (m).  The slide also highlights the relationship between the rank and the presence of free variables or zero rows in the matrix.  The handwritten style suggests a lecture setting.
