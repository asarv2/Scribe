Properties of $dim(V)$

\begin{itemize}
    \item $dim(V) = m$: Any list of $m$ vectors of $V$, $m > dim(V)$, must be linearly dependent.  Then $m \ge dim(V)$.
    \item $dim(V) = n$: Any list of $m$ linearly independent vectors in $V$, $m \le dim(V)$. Then $m \le n$.
    \item $Ptf$: Any homogeneous system of $m$ linear equations with at least one more unknown than equations must have at least one free unknown.  Then $m \le n$.
    \item $dim(V) = n$: The maximum number of linearly independent vectors in $V$ is $n$.
\end{itemize}
