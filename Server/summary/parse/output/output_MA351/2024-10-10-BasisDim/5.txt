Basis is and dimension of $V$: The number of vectors in a (any) defined basis.

The dimension of $V$ is the number of vectors in a basis (any basis).
e.g. $R^2$: $Span\{(1,0), (0,1)\} = R^2$
Hence $dim(R^2) = 2$
Note: There can be many different basis but the dimension is always the same.
