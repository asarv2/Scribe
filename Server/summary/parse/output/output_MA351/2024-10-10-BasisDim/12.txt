Properties of $dim(V)$

\begin{enumerate}
    \item $dim(V) = n$: The dimension of $V$ is the number of vectors in any basis of $V$.
    \item $dim(V) = n$: The minimum number of vectors to span $V$ is $n$.
    \item $dim(V) = n$: Any list of $n$ linearly independent vectors in $V$ spans $V$.
    \item In $n$-dimensional vector space, any basis gives a vector space.
\end{enumerate}
