Slide 8

Let $P_0 = \begin{bmatrix} a_0 \\ b_0 \end{bmatrix}$, $P_1 = \begin{bmatrix} a_1 \\ b_1 \end{bmatrix}, \dots, P_n = \begin{bmatrix} a_n \\ b_n \end{bmatrix}$

Then $P_1 = A P_0$, $P_2 = A P_1 = A^2 P_0$, $P_3 = A P_2 = A^3 P_0$, $\dots$

$P_n = A^n P_0$

$Q: \lim_{n \to \infty} P_n = \lim_{n \to \infty} A^n P_0 = ?$

Ans: Find $\lambda, X$ for $A = \begin{bmatrix} 0.9 & 0.07 \\ 0.1 & 0.93 \end{bmatrix}$

$\det(A - \lambda I) = \det \begin{bmatrix} 0.9 - \lambda & 0.07 \\ 0.1 & 0.93 - \lambda \end{bmatrix}$

$= (0.9 - \lambda)(0.93 - \lambda) - 0.007$

$= \lambda^2 - 1.83 \lambda + 0.83$

$= (\lambda - 1)(\lambda - 0.83)$

$\lambda_1 = 0.83$
