\textbf{Performing the Pivot Operation}

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Identify the pivot element.}  This is the element in the intersection of the leaving row and entering column.
    \item \textbf{Divide the leaving row by the pivot element.} This makes the pivot element equal to 1.
    \item \textbf{Make all other elements in the entering column equal to 0.} Use row operations to achieve this.  This involves subtracting multiples of the pivot row from other rows.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Description:}

This slide details the steps involved in performing a pivot operation within the simplex method.  It's a crucial step in the iterative process of the simplex algorithm. These steps are essential for updating the tableau in each iteration of the simplex method, moving closer to the optimal solution.
