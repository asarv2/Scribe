\textbf{Description:}

This slide demonstrates a system of two linear equations with no solution. The equations are $x + 3y = 7$ and $2x + 6y = 3$.  The slide shows the steps of solving the system using row reduction.  The result is a contradictory equation ($0y = 5$), indicating that the lines represented by the equations are parallel and do not intersect.  The graph visually confirms this, showing two parallel lines in the $xy$-plane. The handwritten style and the use of arrows to indicate steps in the calculation are characteristic of lecture notes.
