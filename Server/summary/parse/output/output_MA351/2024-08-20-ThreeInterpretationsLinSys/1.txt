\textbf{Interpretations of Solving Linear Systems}

\textit{Given:}
\begin{align*}
2x - y &= 4 \\
x + 3y &= 9
\end{align*}

\textbf{Solve by elimination \& substitution:}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item $\implies$ $y = 2x - 4$
    \item $\implies$ $x + 3(2x - 4) = 9$
\end{enumerate}

$7x = 9 + 12 = 21$

Hence $x = 3$, $y = 2(3) - 4 = 2$

\textbf{Solution:} $(x, y) = (3, 2)$, or $(x, y) = (3, 2)$
