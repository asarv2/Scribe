\section*{Vectors in $R^n$}

$\vec{u} = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \end{pmatrix}, \quad \vec{v} = \begin{pmatrix} y_1 \\ y_2 \\ \vdots \\ y_n \end{pmatrix} \in R^n$

\section*{Vector Addition}

$\vec{u} + \vec{v} = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} y_1 \\ y_2 \\ \vdots \\ y_n \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 + y_1 \\ x_2 + y_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n + y_n \end{pmatrix}$

--- Description ---

This slide introduces vector addition in $R^n$.  It defines vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ as $n$-tuples of real numbers.  The key concept is that the sum of two vectors is obtained by adding their corresponding components.  The slide also includes a graphical representation of vector addition using a parallelogram law.  The figure shows two vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ in a 2-dimensional space, originating from the origin.  The sum $\vec{u} + \vec{v}$ is represented by the diagonal of the parallelogram formed by $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$.  The red dashed line represents the vector sum.  The blue and red arrows represent the vectors $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{u}$ respectively.  The slide number is 1 of 14, indicating it's the first in a sequence of linear programming lectures.
