\end{itemize}

    \item $z_1 = 1 - 2 y_1 - 2 y_2 - y_3$

\end{itemize}

\textbf{Description:}

This slide introduces a "Dual Based Phase I Algorithm" for solving a linear programming problem.  It presents the primal problem (maximize $-x_1 + 4x_2$ subject to constraints) and its corresponding dual problem (minimize $-4y_1 + 8y_2 + 7y_3$ subject to constraints).  The slide shows the initial tableau setup for both problems, with the objective function and constraints explicitly written out.  The notation (P) and (D) clearly identify the primal and dual problems.  The final line, "origin not feasible for both," indicates that the origin (where all variables are zero) does not satisfy the constraints of either the primal or dual problem.  This suggests that a Phase I procedure might be necessary to find a feasible starting point for the simplex method. The reference "LVJ p.73" indicates the source of the problem.
