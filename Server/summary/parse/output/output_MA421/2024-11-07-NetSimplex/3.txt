\textbf{Network Simplex Method (Primal and Dual)}

\textit{Network} (N, A)

\textit{Spanning Tree} $T = (N, \tilde{A})$, connected, acyclic

(Very easy to find a spanning tree: [V] p. 253 Ex 14.4F)

(2) Dual How always exists:

$(i,j) \in A$:  $y_j - y_i + \sum z_{ij} = c_{ij}$

$(i,j) \in T$: $y_j - y_i = c_{ij}$, $\sum z_{ij} = 0$

$(i,j) \notin T$: $\sum z_{ij} = c_{ij} - (y_j - y_i)$

(might be infeasible: $z_{ij} > 0$)
