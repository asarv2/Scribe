\textbf{Gomory Cuts}

Adding the Gomory cut to the problem:

$10x_1 - 2x_2 \le 32$
$4x_1 + 10x_2 \le 38$
$-2x_1 + 10x_2 \le 29$
$x_1, x_2 \ge 0$
$x_1, x_2$ integers.
$\frac{5}{54} w_1 + \frac{1}{54} w_2 \le \frac{2}{3}$

\textit{Rounding down the fractional part of $x_1$ and $x_2$:}

$x_1 = \lfloor \frac{11}{3} \rfloor - \frac{5}{54} w_1 - \frac{1}{54} w_2 = 3 - \frac{5}{54} w_1 - \frac{1}{54} w_2$
$x_2 = \lfloor \frac{7}{3} \rfloor + \frac{1}{27} w_1 - \frac{5}{54} w_2 = 2 + \frac{1}{27} w_1 - \frac{5}{54} w_2$

\textit{New slack variable:}

$w_4 = \frac{2}{3} - \frac{5}{54} w_1 - \frac{1}{54} w_2$
