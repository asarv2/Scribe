$\text{LV} \text{ p. } 11$
Set $x_1 = x_2 = x_3 = 0$
$\max \mathcal{Z} = 5x_1 + 4x_2 + 3x_3$
subject to
$2x_1 + 3x_2 + x_3 \le 5$
$4x_1 + x_2 + 2x_3 \le 11$
$3x_1 + 4x_2 + 2x_3 \le 8$
$x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0$

$\omega_1 = 5 - 2x_1 - 3x_2 - x_3$
$\omega_2 = 11 - 4x_1 - x_2 - 2x_3$
$\omega_3 = 8 - 3x_1 - 4x_2 - 2x_3$
$x_1, x_2, x_3, \omega_1, \omega_2, \omega_3 \ge 0$

The context suggests this is a preliminary step in the Simplex method, where the algorithm begins at a specific vertex (the origin) and then proceeds to find a better solution by moving to another vertex.
