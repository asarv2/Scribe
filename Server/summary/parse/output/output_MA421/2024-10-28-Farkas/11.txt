\textbf{[mg] Farkas Lemma (Other Versions)}

6.4.3 Proposition (Farkas lemma in three variants). Let $A$ be a real
matrix with $m$ rows and $n$ columns, and let $b \in \mathbb{R}^m$ be a vector.

(i) The system $Ax = b$ has a nonnegative solution $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$
with $x \ge 0$ if and only if every $y \in \mathbb{R}^m$ with $y^T A \ge 0^T$ also satisfies $y^T b \ge 0$.

(ii) The system $Ax \le b$ has a nonnegative solution $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$
with $x \ge 0$ if and only if every $y \in \mathbb{R}^m$ with $y^T A \ge 0^T$ also satisfies $y^T b \ge 0$.

(iii) The system $Ax \le b$ has a solution $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ if and only if every $y \in \mathbb{R}^m$ with $y^T A = 0^T$ also satisfies $y^T b \ge 0$.

\begin{itemize}
    \item $Ax \le b$ has no solution iff there is $y \ge 0$ s.t. $y^T A \ge 0^T$ and $y^T b < 0$.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Proof of (iii)}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Assume $Ax \le b$ has no solution.
    \item Then there exists $y \ge 0$ such that $y^T A \ge 0^T$ and $y^T b < 0$.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Conclusion}

Thus, $Ax \le b$ has no solution iff there is $y \ge 0$ s.t. $y^T A \ge 0^T$ and $y^T b < 0$.
