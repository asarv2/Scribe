\textbf{Network Flow (LV) Chapter 14)}

(1) $b_i > 0$ (Supply)  $b_i < 0$ (Demand)  $\sum_{i \in N} b_i = 0$

(2) $X_{ij}$ = amount transported from $i$ to $j$

(3) $C_{ij}$ = cost of transportation from $i$ to $j$

(4) $\text{min} \sum_{(i,j) \in \mathcal{A}} C_{ij} X_{ij}$


\textbf{Description:}

This slide continues the Network Flow discussion, likely from the previous slide.  It defines key variables and constraints for a network flow problem, likely a transportation problem.

* **(1) $b_i$**: This represents the net flow at each node.  Positive values indicate supply, negative values indicate demand.  The equation $\sum_{i \in N} b_i = 0$ is a crucial constraint, ensuring that the total supply equals the total demand in the network.
* **(2) $X_{ij}$**: This variable represents the amount of flow along the arc from node $i$ to node $j$.
* **(3) $C_{ij}$**: This variable represents the cost of transporting one unit of flow along the arc from node $i$ to node $j$.
* **(4) $\text{min} \sum_{(i,j) \in \mathcal{A}} C_{ij} X_{ij}$**: This is the objective function, aiming to minimize the total cost of transporting all the flow in the network.
