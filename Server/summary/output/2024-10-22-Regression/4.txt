```latex

\textbf{L\textsuperscript{2} Regression (Least Square)}

\begin{equation*}
\min_{(x_1, \dots, x_n)} \sum_{i=1}^m \left| (b_i - \sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij} x_j) \right|^2
\end{equation*}

\begin{equation*}
\min \left\| b - AX \right\|_2^2
\end{equation*}

$AX = b$ (might not be solvable)

$A^T A \hat{X} = A^T b$ (normal equation, always solvable)

$\hat{X} = (A^T A)^{-1} A^T b$ (if $(A^T A)^{-1}$ exists)

\end{document}
```

It shows the objective function for minimizing the sum of squared errors between the observed values ($b_i$) and the predicted values ($\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij} x_j$). The slide highlights the normal equation ($A^T A \hat{X} = A^T b$) as a way to solve for the optimal parameters ($\hat{X}$), which is always solvable.  The final equation shows the solution for $\hat{X}$ in terms of matrix operations, requiring the inverse of $(A^T A)$.  The handwritten style and mathematical notation indicate a lecture setting.
