Slide 5

\textbf{Production Planning}

\newline
\textit{[m\&p] p.16}

\newline
$\min 50 \sum_{i=1}^{12} (Y_i + Z_i) + 20 \sum_{i=1}^{12} S_i$

\newline
s.t.
\newline
$X_i + S_{i-1} - S_i = d_i$  \hspace{0.5cm} $i=1,2,\dots,12$
\newline
$X_i - X_{i-1} = Y_i - Z_i$ \hspace{0.5cm} $i=1,2,\dots,12$
\newline
$X_0 = 0$
\newline
$S_0 = 0$
\newline
$S_{12} = 0$
\newline
$X_i, Y_i, Z_i, S_i \ge 0$ \hspace{0.5cm} $i=1,2,\dots,12$


\textbf{Description:}

This slide presents the complete linear programming formulation for the ice cream production planning problem.  The objective function, $\min 50 \sum_{i=1}^{12} (Y_i + Z_i) + 20 \sum_{i=1}^{12} S_i$, now explicitly includes the costs of changing production levels ($Y_i$ and $Z_i$) and storage ($S_i$).  The constraints are clearly defined, including the initial conditions ($X_0 = 0$, $S_0 = 0$, $S_{12} = 0$) and the non-negativity constraints for all variables.  The constraints ensure that production levels and storage levels are consistent with the predicted demand for each month.  This complete formulation is ready for solution using linear programming techniques.
