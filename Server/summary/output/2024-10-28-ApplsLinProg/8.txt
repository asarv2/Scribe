Slide 8

\textbf{Reformulated Problem}

\newline
$\min 25 \sum_{i=1}^{12} Y_i + (17.5 \times 12) X$

\newline
s.t.
\newline
$a_i - X \le Y_i$
\newline
$0 \le Y_i$
\newline
$X \ge 0$


\textbf{Description:}

This slide presents a reformulation of the workforce planning problem from the previous slide.  The objective function now explicitly minimizes the total cost, which is the sum of the cost of temporary employees ($25 \sum_{i=1}^{12} Y_i$) and the cost of regular employees ($17.5 \times 12 \times X$).  The constraints are also presented, ensuring that the number of hours of temporary employees ($Y_i$) is greater than or equal to the difference between the required labor hours ($a_i$) and the number of regular employees ($X$).  The non-negativity constraints for $Y_i$ and $X$ are also included.  This reformulation is likely a crucial step in solving the problem using standard linear programming techniques.
