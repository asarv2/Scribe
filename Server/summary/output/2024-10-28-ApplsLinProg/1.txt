\textbf{Description:}

This is the first slide of a 37-slide presentation on Linear Programming.  It introduces a case study involving Icicle Works Ltd., an ice cream manufacturer, needing to create a production plan for the upcoming year.  The slide presents a bar graph illustrating the predicted monthly sales (in tons) of ice cream for the year, from January to December.  The $d_i$ notation indicates the monthly demands.  The handwritten notes clearly outline the problem context and the data used for the production planning.  The slide number and page number are also included.
