\textbf{THE GRAPHIC METHOD}

The geometric interpretation of linear programming leads to a geometric procedure for solving
LP problems with two variables. For illustration, let us consider the problem

maximize $\quad x_1 + x_2$

subject to $\quad 2x_1 + x_2 \le 14$
$\quad -x_1 + 2x_2 \le 8$
$\quad 2x_1 - x_2 \le 10$
$\quad x_1, x_2 \ge 0$.

Its region of feasibility is shown in Figure 17.8, together with the line representing all the points

$x_1 + x_2 = 10$
