\textbf{Description:}

This slide continues the discussion of the maximum flow problem. It introduces the concept of a cut (denoted by $C$) in the network. The diagram shows a more complex network with multiple nodes and edges. The purple lines highlight a specific cut in the network. The formula $N = C \cup C^c$, $s \in C$ ($t \in C^c$) defines the set $N$ as the union of the cut set $C$ and its complement $C^c$, with the source $s$ in $C$ and the sink $t$ in $C^c$. The formula $K(C) = \sum_{i \in C, j \notin C} u_{ij}$ calculates the capacity of the cut $C$ by summing the capacities of all edges that cross the cut from nodes in $C$ to nodes outside of $C$. The new addition is the statement "(any) Flow ($s \to t$) $\le$ (any) $K(C)$", which is a key part of the max-flow min-cut theorem.
