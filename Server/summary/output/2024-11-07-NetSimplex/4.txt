\textbf{Network Simplex Method (Primal and Dual)}

\textit{Network} (N, A)

\textit{Spanning Tree} $T = (N, \tilde{A})$, connected, acyclic

(Very easy to find a spanning tree: [V] p. 253 Ex 14.4F)

(3) $T + one \ arc \implies$ there is a cycle

$T - one \ arc \implies$ tree becomes disconnected

(Bertsmas-Tsitsikis : Intro. Lin. Opt. p. 279)

\textit{The network simplex algorithm is widely used in practice, and is included in many commercial optimization codes, due to its simplicity and efficiency. In particular, it tends to run an order of magnitude faster than a general purpose simplex code applied to a network flow problem.}
