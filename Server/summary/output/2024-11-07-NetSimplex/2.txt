\textbf{Network Simplex Method (Primal and Dual)}

\textit{Network} (N, A)

\textit{Spanning Tree} $T = (N, \tilde{A})$, connected, acyclic

(Very easy to find a spanning tree: [V] p. 253 Ex 14.4F)

(1) Primal flow always exists:

$\tilde{B}\tilde{X} = \tilde{b}$, $x_{ij} = 0$, $(i,j) \in A \setminus T$

Start from a leaf node and go inwards.

(Might be infeasible, Some $x_{ij} < 0$)
