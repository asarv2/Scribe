\textbf{Description:}

This slide continues the Network Flow discussion, likely from the previous two slides. It introduces the concept of "balanced equations" at each node in the network.

* **Balanced eqn:**  The equation $\sum_{i} x_{ik} + b_k = \sum_{j} x_{kj}$  states that the sum of flows entering a node ($k$) plus the supply at that node ($b_k$) must equal the sum of flows leaving the node.  This is a fundamental conservation law for flow networks.  The equation is further simplified to $\sum_{i} x_{ik} - \sum_{j} x_{kj} = -b_k$.
* **Objective Function and Constraints:** The slide also restates the objective function (minimizing total cost) and the constraints ($AX = -b$, $X \ge 0$) from the previous slide.  The $A$ matrix likely represents the incidence matrix of the network, defining the flow relationships between nodes.

The slide likely connects the network flow problem to a standard linear programming formulation.  The notation suggests that the lecture is covering how to formulate and solve network flow problems as linear programming problems.  The diagram shows a simplified network with a node $k$ and flows into and out of it.
