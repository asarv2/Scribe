\textbf{Spanning Trees and Bases}

We can now apply the primal simplex method to the problem.  The steps are as follows:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Identify the entering variable.  This is the variable corresponding to the arc with the most negative reduced cost.
    \item Identify the leaving variable.  This is the variable corresponding to the arc that is part of the cycle formed by the entering variable and the spanning tree.
    \item Update the spanning tree by replacing the leaving variable with the entering variable.
    \item Update the primal and dual variables.
    \item Repeat steps 1-4 until all dual slacks are nonnegative.
\end{enumerate}
