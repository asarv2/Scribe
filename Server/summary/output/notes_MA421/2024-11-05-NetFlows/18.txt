\textbf{Spanning Trees and Bases}

Dual Flow: $y_j - y_i + z_{ij} = c_{ij}$  $(z_{ij} \ge 0)$

Dual Flow for Spanning Tree ($T$):
\begin{itemize}
    \item $x_{ij} \neq 0 \implies z_{ij} = 0$  $(i,j) \in T$
    \item $x_{ij} = 0 \implies y_j - y_i = c_{ij}$  $(i,j) \notin T$
\end{itemize}

Complementary Slackness: $x_{ij} z_{ij} = 0$
