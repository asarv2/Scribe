\textbf{Branch-and-Bound}

maximize $17x_1 + 12x_2$
subject to $10x_1 + 7x_2 \le 40$
$x_1 + x_2 \le 5$
$x_1, x_2 \ge 0$
$x_1, x_2$ integers.

\textit{Graph of feasible region and optimal solution} (a plot is shown)

$\vec{x}^* = (5/3, 10/3)$

$x_1 \le 1 \quad \text{or} \quad x_1 \ge 2$ $\implies$ 2 sub-problems, with new constraints

$P_0$: $x_1 = 1.67, x_2 = 3.33$

$\implies \zeta = 65$

$P_2$: $x_1 \ge 2, x_2 \ge 2.86$
