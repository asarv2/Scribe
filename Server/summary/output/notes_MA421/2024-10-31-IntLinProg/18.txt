\textbf{Gomory Cuts}

From the optimal dictionary for the relaxed problem, we can derive a Gomory cut.
Consider the equation for $x_1$:

$x_1 = \frac{11}{3} - \frac{5}{54} w_1 - \frac{1}{54} w_2$

Since $x_1$ must be an integer, the fractional part of $\frac{11}{3}$ is $\frac{2}{3}$.
The fractional part of $\frac{5}{54}$ is $\frac{5}{54}$.
The fractional part of $\frac{1}{54}$ is $\frac{1}{54}$.

Therefore, the Gomory cut is:

$\frac{5}{54} w_1 + \frac{1}{54} w_2 \le \frac{2}{3}$

\textit{Adding the Gomory cut to the problem:}

$10x_1 - 2x_2 \le 32$
$4x_1 + 10x_2 \le 38$
$-2x_1 + 10x_2 \le 29$
$x_1, x_2 \ge 0$
$x_1, x_2$ integers.
$\frac{5}{54} w_1 + \frac{1}{54} w_2 \le \frac{2}{3}$
