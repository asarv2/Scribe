\textbf{Description:}

This slide continues the discussion of the maximum flow problem. It now states the max-flow min-cut theorem. The diagram shows a network with multiple nodes and edges. The purple lines highlight a specific cut in the network. The theorem states that the maximum possible flow from the source ($s$) to the sink ($t$) in a network is equal to the minimum capacity of any cut ($C$) in the network.  The equation (max) Flow ($s \to t$) = (min) $K(C)$ is written below the diagram, summarizing the theorem.
