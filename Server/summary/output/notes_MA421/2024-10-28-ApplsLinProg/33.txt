\textbf{Sparse Solutions of Linear System}

\newline
\textit{[EMG] p. 167}

\newline
Sparse solution of underdetermined system of linear equations

\newline
Given an $m \times n$ matrix $A$ with $m < n$, a vector $b \in \mathbb{R}^m$, and an integer $r$, find an $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ such that $Ax = b$ and $|supp(x)| \le r$

\newline
if one exists.

\newline
($|supp(x)| = \# \{i: x_i \ne 0\}$)

\newline
Data Compression:

\newline
$AX = b$

\newline
$x_1 U_1 + x_2 U_2 + \dots + x_n U_n = b$

\newline
$\underline{x} = \underline{x} - x$

\newline
$\underline{x}$ = coordinates of $b$ w.r.t. cols of $A$
