\textbf{Geometric Interpretation of Dual Constraints}

\vspace{0.5cm}

The dual constraints can be interpreted geometrically as half-spaces in the $x_1$-$x_2$ plane.  The inequality $(2y_1 - y_2 + 2y_3) x_1 + (y_1 + 2y_2 - y_3) x_2 \le r(y_1, y_2, y_3)$ defines a half-space.  The optimal solution to the dual problem lies at the intersection of the constraints.
