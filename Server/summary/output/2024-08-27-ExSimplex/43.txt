Slide 42

Optimal solution to the auxiliary problem: $x_1 = 2$, $x_2 = 1$, $x_0 = 0$, $\overline{\mathcal{Z}} = -5$

Since $x_0 = 0$, this is a feasible solution to the original problem.

Substituting $x_0 = 0$ into the equations for $x_1$ and $x_2$ from the auxiliary problem dictionary, we get:

$x_1 = 2$
$x_2 = 1$

$\max \mathcal{Z} = -2x_1 - x_2 = -2(2) - 1 = -5$
