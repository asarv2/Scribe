\textbf{Description:}

This slide extends the graphical analysis of the linear programming problem from the previous slide.  It now labels the vertices of the feasible region (the area satisfying all constraints) with their coordinates, explicitly showing the values of the slack variables ($w_1$, $w_2$, $w_3$) at each vertex.  The point E is labeled as "not feasible" because it lies outside the feasible region.  The vertices are labeled with their corresponding coordinates in terms of the original variables ($x_1$, $x_2$) and the slack variables ($w_1$, $w_2$, $w_3$).  The context suggests this is a crucial step in the graphical method for solving linear programming problems, identifying the possible optimal solutions within the feasible region.
