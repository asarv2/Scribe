Auxiliary Problem

$\max \overline{\mathcal{Z}} = -x_0$
subject to
$-x_1 + x_2 - x_0 \le -1$
$-x_1 - 2x_2 - x_0 \le -2$
$x_2 - x_0 \le 1$
$x_1, x_2, x_0 \ge 0$

(1) Must be feasible: choose $x_0$ large enough
(2) Original problem is feasible

$\max \mathcal{Z} = 0$
