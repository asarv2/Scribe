Auxiliary Problem

$-x_1 + x_2 - x_0 \le -1$

$x_1, x_2, x_0 \ge 0$

(1) Must be feasible: choose $x_0$ large enough

$x_1, x_2, x_0, \omega_1, \omega_2, \omega_3 \ge 0$
