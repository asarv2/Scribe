**Description:**

This slide builds upon the previous one, further elaborating on the transportation problem's constraints.  The key addition is the introduction of a "dummy node" ($d$).  A red arrow now connects the original node $i$ to the dummy node $d$, labeled $x_{id}$.  Similarly, a red arrow connects each demand node $d_j$ to the dummy node $d$, labeled $x_{d_j}$.  These new variables represent the excess supply or unmet demand.  The equations below the diagram now incorporate these dummy variables.  The equation $\sum_{j} x_{ij} + x_{id} = s_i$  indicates that the total flow out of each supply node, plus the flow to the dummy node, must equal the supply.  The equation $\sum_{i} x_{ij} - d_j + x_{d_j} = d_j$  indicates that the total flow into each demand node, minus the demand, plus the flow from the dummy node, must equal the demand.  The addition of the dummy node and these equations ensure that all supply is accounted for and all demand is met, even if there's excess supply or unmet demand.  The constraint $x_{id}, x_{d_j} \ge 0$ ensures that these flows are non-negative.  This is a crucial step in formulating the transportation problem as a linear program.
