\section*{Theorem 10.1}
$S$ is convex $\iff$
it contains all conv. comb. of points in $S$

\textbf{Proof} $\Leftarrow$
Suppose $S$ contain all conv. comb. of pts in $S$
Then clearly, for any $z_1, z_2 \in S$
$\lambda z_1 + (1-\lambda) z_2 \in S$
$0 \le \lambda \le 1$
$\implies S$ is convex
