\section*{Convex Analysis [V] Chapter 10}

\subsection*{Caratheodory Theorem}
If $z \in \text{Conv}(S)$, then $z$ can be written as a convex combination of at most $m+1$ points from $S$.

\textbf{Proof:}
Consider the LP:
\begin{align*}
\max \ c^T x \\
\text{s.t.} \ Ax = z \\
x_i \ge 0, \ \sum_{j=1}^n x_j = 1 \\
\end{align*}
where $x = [x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n]^T$, $A = [z_1, z_2, \dots, z_n]$, and $z_j \in S$.

This is feasible, and hence has a basic opt. soln with $m+1$ basic variables.
