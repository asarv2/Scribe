\textbf{Description:}

This slide continues the proof of the Strong Duality Theorem for linear programming.  It shows the primal objective function ($\mathcal{S}$) rewritten at the optimal solution of the primal problem (P) in terms of non-basic variables ($x_j^*$) and basic variables ($w_i^*$).  The notation $c_j^*$, $x_j^*$, and $w_i^*$ represent the optimal values of the primal variables and slack variables.  The equation shows that the optimal value of the primal objective function ($\mathcal{S}^*$) is equal to the sum of the optimal values of the non-basic variables and a term involving the slack variables.  The final equation shows the relationship between the optimal primal objective function value and the optimal dual objective function value.  This is a crucial step in the proof, as it demonstrates the equality of the optimal values of the primal and dual objective functions.
