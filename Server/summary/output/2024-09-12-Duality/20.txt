\newline
Four and Only Four Possibilities
\newline
(1) (P) has an optimal solution $\implies$ (D) also has an optimal solution, $\mathcal{S}^* = \mathcal{S}^*$, duality gap = 0
\newline
(2) (P) is feasible but unbounded $\implies$ (D) must be infeasible, $\mathcal{S}(x) \le \mathcal{S}(y) \implies +\infty$ (min empty set = $+\infty$)
\newline
(3) (D) is feasible but unbounded $\implies$ (P) must be infeasible, $\mathcal{S}(x) \le \mathcal{S}(y) \implies -\infty$ (max empty set = $-\infty$)
\newline
(4) Both (P) and (D) are infeasible
\newline
Example:
\newline
(P) maximize $2x_1 - x_2$
\newline
subject to $x_1 - x_2 \le 1$
\newline
$-x_1 + x_2 \le -2$
\newline
$x_1, x_2 \ge 0$
\newline
(D) minimize $y_1 - 2y_2$
\newline
subject to $y_1 - y_2 \ge 2$
\newline
$-y_1 + y_2 \ge -1$
\newline
$y_1, y_2 \ge 0$
\newline
(P) and (D) are inconsistent
