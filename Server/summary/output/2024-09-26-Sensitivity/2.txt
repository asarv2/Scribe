\textbf{Description:}

This slide presents the optimal solution (or a portion of it) to the linear programming problem from the previous slide.  The equations show the relationships between the original variables ($x_1$, $x_2$, $x_3$, $x_5$) and the slack variables ($x_4$, $x_6$).  The variable $\xi$ likely represents the objective function value at the optimal solution.  The expressions for $x_1$, $x_3$, $x_5$ are in terms of the other variables, indicating the basic variables and their relationships to the non-basic variables.  This is a common form of expressing the solution in linear programming, often used in sensitivity analysis to understand how changes in the problem parameters affect the optimal solution.
