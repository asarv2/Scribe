\section*{Sensitivity and Parametric Analysis}

what if
\begin{align*}
\text{maximize} \quad & 5x_1 + 4x_2 + 3x_3 \\
\text{subject to} \quad & 2x_1 + 3x_2 + x_3 \le 5 + \Delta b_1 \\
& 4x_1 + x_2 + 2x_3 \le 11 + \Delta b_2 \\
& 3x_1 + 4x_2 + 2x_3 \le 8 + \Delta b_3 \\
& x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0
\end{align*}

\vspace{1cm}

\textbf{Description:}

This slide presents a modified linear programming problem, focusing on the sensitivity of the constraint values.  The original constraints, $2x_1 + 3x_2 + x_3 \le 5$, $4x_1 + x_2 + 2x_3 \le 11$, and $3x_1 + 4x_2 + 2x_3 \le 8$, have been altered to include the parameters $\Delta b_1$, $\Delta b_2$, and $\Delta b_3$.  These parameters represent potential changes in the right-hand side (RHS) values of the constraints.  This formulation is typical of a parametric analysis in linear programming, where the constraint values are allowed to vary, and the impact on the optimal solution is investigated.  The question "what if" suggests an exploration of how different values of $\Delta b_i$ affect the optimal solution.  The slide is preparing for a discussion on the range of changes in the constraint values that will not change the optimal solution.
