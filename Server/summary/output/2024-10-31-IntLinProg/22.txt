\textbf{Gomory Cuts}

Adding the Gomory cut to the problem:

$10x_1 - 2x_2 \le 32$
$4x_1 + 10x_2 \le 38$
$-2x_1 + 10x_2 \le 29$
$x_1, x_2 \ge 0$
$x_1, x_2$ integers.
$\frac{5}{54} w_1 + \frac{1}{54} w_2 \le \frac{2}{3}$

\textit{Rounding down the fractional part of $x_1$ and $x_2$:}

$x_1 = \lfloor \frac{11}{3} \rfloor - \frac{5}{54} w_1 - \frac{1}{54} w_2 = 3 - \frac{5}{54} w_1 - \frac{1}{54} w_2$
$x_2 = \lfloor \frac{7}{3} \rfloor + \frac{1}{27} w_1 - \frac{5}{54} w_2 = 2 + \frac{1}{27} w_1 - \frac{5}{54} w_2$

\textit{New slack variable:}

$w_4 = \frac{2}{3} - \frac{5}{54} w_1 - \frac{1}{54} w_2$

\textit{Optimal dictionary after adding the cut:}

$\qquad \xi = \frac{289}{5} - \frac{14}{5} w_4 - \frac{13}{10} w_2$

$\qquad x_1 = 3 - \frac{1}{5} w_4 - \frac{1}{10} w_2$

$\qquad x_2 = \frac{13}{5} + \frac{2}{5} w_4 - \frac{1}{10} w_2$

$\qquad w_3 = 9 - 6 w_4 + \frac{1}{5} w_2$

$\qquad w_1 = \frac{36}{5} + \frac{54}{5} w_4 - \frac{1}{5} w_2$

\textit{Further analysis of $x_2$:}

$x_2 = \frac{13}{5} + \frac{2}{5} w_4 - \frac{1}{10} w_2 = 2\frac{3}{5} + \frac{2}{5} w_4 - \frac{1}{10} w_2$

$x_2 - 2\frac{3}{5} = \frac{2}{5} w_4 - \frac{1}{10} w_2$

\textit{New Gomory cut from $x_2$:}

$\frac{2}{5} w_4 + \frac{1}{10} w_2 \le \frac{3}{5}$
