\section*{Simplex in Matrix Notation}

\begin{align*}
\max \ \ & \mathbf{z}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{c}^T \mathbf{x} \\
\text{s.t.} \ \ & \mathbf{A} \mathbf{x} \le \mathbf{b} \\
& \mathbf{x} \ge \mathbf{0}
\end{align*}
$\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n$, $\mathbf{c} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ \\
$\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $\mathbf{b} \in \mathbb{R}^m$

\vspace{1cm}

\begin{align*}
\max \ \ & \mathbf{z}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{c}^T \mathbf{x} \\
\text{s.t.} \ \ & \mathbf{w} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{A} \mathbf{x} \\
& \mathbf{x}, \mathbf{w} \ge \mathbf{0}
\end{align*}
$\mathbf{w} \in \mathbb{R}^m$

\vspace{1cm}

\begin{align*}
\max \ \ & \mathbf{z}(\mathbf{x}) = \overline{\mathbf{z}} + \mathbf{c}^T \mathbf{x} \\
\text{s.t.} \ \ & \overline{\mathbf{w}} = \overline{\mathbf{b}} - \mathbf{A} \mathbf{x} \\
& \mathbf{x}, \overline{\mathbf{w}} \ge \mathbf{0}
\end{align*}

\textbf{Description:}

This slide continues the discussion of linear programming in matrix notation, focusing on the transformation of the problem for simplex iterations.  It introduces a new formulation, where the objective function now includes a constant term $\overline{\mathbf{z}}$.  The constraint $\mathbf{w} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{A} \mathbf{x}$ is replaced by $\overline{\mathbf{w}} = \overline{\mathbf{b}} - \mathbf{A} \mathbf{x}$.  The notation suggests a transition to a specific form of the simplex method, where the initial solution is not necessarily all zeros. The arrows and text "Simplex iterates" indicate that this is a step in the simplex algorithm.  The introduction of the overlined variables ($\overline{\mathbf{z}}$, $\overline{\mathbf{w}}$, $\overline{\mathbf{b}}$) suggests a shift from the initial problem formulation to a form suitable for the iterative steps of the simplex method.  The slide is likely part of a lecture explaining how the simplex method works in matrix form.
