\section*{Example}

Maximize $4x_1 + 3x_2$ subject to:
\begin{align*}
x_1 - x_2 &\le 1 \\
2x_1 - x_2 &\le 3 \\
x_1, x_2 &\le 5 \\
x_1, x_2 &\ge 0
\end{align*}

The matrix $A$ is given by:
\[
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & -1 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\
2 & -1 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
\]

$B = \{3, 4, 5\}$, $N = \{1, 2\}$
