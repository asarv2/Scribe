\section*{Basic and Non-Basic Variables}

$\mathbf{x} = \begin{pmatrix}
x_1 \\
\vdots \\
x_n \\
w_1 \\
\vdots \\
w_m
\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}
\mathbf{x}_B \\
\mathbf{x}_N
\end{pmatrix}$

$\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{A} & \mathbf{I} \end{bmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{x}_B \\ \mathbf{x}_N \end{pmatrix} = \mathbf{b}$

$\mathbf{A}_B \mathbf{x}_B + \mathbf{A}_N \mathbf{x}_N + \mathbf{w} = \mathbf{b}$

$B = $ basic variables columns of $[\mathbf{A} \ \mathbf{I}]$

$N = $ non-basic variables columns of $[\mathbf{A} \ \mathbf{I}]$

\textbf{Description:}

This slide further clarifies the concept of basic and non-basic variables in the context of the simplex method.  It explicitly shows how the augmented matrix representation of the constraints, $\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{A} & \mathbf{I} \end{bmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{x}_B \\ \mathbf{x}_N \end{pmatrix} = \mathbf{b}$, is partitioned into basic and non-basic variable columns.  The equation $\mathbf{A}_B \mathbf{x}_B + \mathbf{A}_N \mathbf{x}_N + \mathbf{w} = \mathbf{b}$ demonstrates how the constraints are expressed in terms of these partitions.  Crucially, the slide defines $B$ and $N$ as sets of indices corresponding to the basic and non-basic variable columns in the augmented matrix.  This is a critical step in the simplex algorithm, as it allows for efficient manipulation of the constraints during the iterative process.  The slide is likely part of a lecture explaining the simplex method's underlying structure and how the constraints are handled in the algorithm.
