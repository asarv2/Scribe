\section*{Simplex Iterations in Matrix Form}

\begin{align*}
\max \ \ &\mathcal{S}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{c}^T \mathbf{x} \\
\text{s.t.} \ \ &\mathbf{w} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{A} \mathbf{x} \\
&\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{w} \ge \mathbf{0}
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
\max \ \ &\mathcal{S}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{c}_B^T \mathbf{x}_B + \mathbf{c}_N^T \mathbf{x}_N \\
\text{s.t.} \ \ &\mathbf{x}_B = B^{-1} \mathbf{b} - B^{-1} N \mathbf{x}_N \\
&\mathbf{x}_B, \mathbf{x}_N \ge \mathbf{0}
\end{align*}

$\mathcal{S}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{c}_B^T B^{-1} \mathbf{b} + (\mathbf{c}_N^T - \mathbf{c}_B^T B^{-1} N) \mathbf{x}_N = \overline{\mathcal{Z}} + \mathbf{c}_N^T \mathbf{x}_N$

\textbf{Description:}

This slide presents the objective function in matrix form, specifically for a simplex iteration.  It shows how the objective function ($\mathcal{S}(\mathbf{x})$) is expressed in terms of basic ($\mathbf{x}_B$) and non-basic ($\mathbf{x}_N$) variables.  The key is the substitution of the expression for $\mathbf{x}_B$ from the constraint equation into the objective function.  The result, $\mathcal{S}(\mathbf{x}) = \overline{\mathcal{Z}} + \mathbf{c}_N^T \mathbf{x}_N$, isolates the constant term ($\overline{\mathcal{Z}}$) and the terms dependent on the non-basic variables ($\mathbf{x}_N$).  This form is crucial for the simplex method, as it allows for efficient calculation of the objective function value at each iteration and for determining the next variable to enter the basis.  The notation $B$, $N$, $\mathbf{c}_B$, $\mathbf{c}_N$, and $B^{-1}$ are all matrix representations used in the simplex algorithm.  The slide is likely part of a lecture explaining the simplex method's underlying structure and how the objective function is updated during iterations.
