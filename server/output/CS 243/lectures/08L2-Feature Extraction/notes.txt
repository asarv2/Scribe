Consider shifting the window $W$ by $(u, v)$ \\
How do the pixels in $W$ change? \\

$\phi_1 = [I(0+u,0+v), I(0+u,1+v), \dots, I(n+u,n+v)]$ \\
Write pixels in window as a vector: \\
$E(u,v) = \|\phi_0 - \phi_1\|^2 = \sum_{(x,y) \in W} (I(x,y) - I(x+u, y+v))^2$

Consider shifting the window $W$ by $(u, v)$ \\
How do the pixels in $W$ change? \\
\begin{itemize}
