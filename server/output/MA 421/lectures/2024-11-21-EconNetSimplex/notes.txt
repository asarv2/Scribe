\text{In the second step of each iteration, we put ourselves in the role of a competitor:} \\
\text{would it pay to buy the commodity at some node } i \text{, ship it along an arc } ij \text{ and sell at} \\
\text{the node } j \text{? In our example, the answer is affirmative for several different arcs } ij \text{. For} \\
\text{instance, we could buy at the node 2 for } y_2 = 23 \text{ and ship along the arc 25 for } c_{25} = \\
37 \text{: the total expense of } y_2 + c_{25} = 60 \text{ compares favorably with the selling price of} \\
y_5 = 62 \text{. In general, this second step consists of choosing an arc } ij \text{ such that} \\
y_i + c_{ij} < y_j \\
\overrightarrow{\text{non-tree arc}} \\

y_j - y_i + z_{ij} = c_{ij} \quad i.e. \quad y_j - y_i > c_{ij}
