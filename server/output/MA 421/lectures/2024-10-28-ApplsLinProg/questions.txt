QUESTION 1
According to the production planning model presented, what does the variable \(x_i\) represent?
A. The demand for ice cream in month \(i\)
B. The surplus inventory at the end of month \(i\)
C. The production level in month \(i\)
D. The cost of changing production levels between consecutive months
E. The cost of storing surplus inventory

ANSWER: C

EXPLANATION: 
A. Answer A is incorrect because \(d_i\) represents the demand for ice cream in month \(i\).
B. Answer B is incorrect because \(s_i\) represents the surplus inventory at the end of month \(i\).
C. Answer C is correct because \(x_i\) is defined as the production at month \(i\).
D. Answer D is incorrect because the cost of changing production levels is represented by \(50|x_i - x_{i-1}|\).
E. Answer E is incorrect because the cost of storing surplus inventory is represented by \(20s_i\).

QUESTION 2
In the production planning model, what is the purpose of the constraint \(x_i + s_{i-1} = d_i + s_i\)?
A. To minimize the total production cost
B. To ensure that production plus beginning inventory equals demand plus ending inventory
C. To maximize the surplus inventory at the end of each month
D. To minimize the cost of changing production levels
E. To balance the average demand with the production level

ANSWER: B

EXPLANATION: 
A. Answer A is incorrect because the objective function minimizes the total cost, not this constraint.
B. Answer B is correct because the constraint ensures that the production in month \(i\) plus the surplus from the previous month equals the demand in month \(i\) plus the surplus at the end of month \(i\).
C. Answer C is incorrect because the constraint is about balancing inventory and demand, not maximizing surplus.
D. Answer D is incorrect because the cost of changing production levels is part of the objective function, not this constraint.
E. Answer E is incorrect because the constraint is about balancing inventory and demand, not the average demand.

QUESTION 3
What does the term \(\sum_{i=1}^{12} 50|x_i - x_{i-1}|\) represent in the objective function of the production planning model?
A. The total cost of storing surplus inventory
B. The total demand for ice cream over 12 months
C. The total cost of changing production levels between consecutive months
D. The total production cost over 12 months
E. The average monthly demand

ANSWER: C

EXPLANATION: 
A. Answer A is incorrect because the cost of storing surplus inventory is represented by \(\sum_{i=1}^{12} 20 s_i\).
B. Answer B is incorrect because the total demand is represented by \(\sum_{i=1}^{12} d_i\), not this term.
C. Answer C is correct because the term represents the cost of changing production levels, where 50 is the cost per unit change.
D. Answer D is incorrect because the total production cost is not directly represented by this term alone.
E. Answer E is incorrect because the average monthly demand is not directly represented by this term.
