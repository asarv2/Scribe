The entire expression is then a convex combination of two elements of $S$, and thus also belongs to $S$. This argument can be generalized for any $n$, completing the proof. The red "$\in S$" emphasizes that the convex combination of $z_1$ and $z_2$ is in $S$. The arrows indicate the flow of logic in the inductive step.
