C. The duality theorem is proven using other techniques.

This is a theoretical justification for algorithms that focus on extreme points, like the simplex method.
C. Finding the dual problem is related to duality theory, not directly to Carathéodory's Theorem.
D. The number of iterations in the simplex method depends on the specific algorithm and the problem instance, not directly on Carathéodory's Theorem.
E. The simplex method is not always efficient in the worst case; the Klee-Minty cube is a counterexample.
