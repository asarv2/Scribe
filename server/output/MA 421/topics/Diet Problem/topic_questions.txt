QUESTION 1
In the context of the diet problem, what does a non-negative slack variable represent?
A. The amount by which a nutrient constraint is exceeded.
B. The cost of the optimal diet.
C. The minimum amount of a nutrient required.
D. The amount by which a nutrient constraint is not met.
E. The price of a food item.

ANSWER: A

EXPLANATION:
A. A non-negative slack variable in a diet problem represents the surplus of a particular nutrient in the optimal diet, meaning the amount by which the nutrient constraint is exceeded.
B. The cost of the optimal diet is represented by the objective function value, not a slack variable.
C. The minimum amount of a nutrient is part of the constraint, not the slack variable.
D. A non-negative slack variable represents the surplus, not the shortfall.  A shortfall would be represented by a surplus variable (which would be non-positive).
E. The price of a food item is a coefficient in the objective function or constraints, not a slack variable.


QUESTION 2

B. The total number of food items consumed.
C. The minimum cost of the diet that satisfies the nutritional requirements.

E. The number of constraints in the problem.

ANSWER: C

EXPLANATION:

B. The number of food items is a variable in the problem, not the objective.
C. The objective function in a diet problem usually aims to minimize the total cost of the diet while meeting all nutritional requirements.
D. Maximum nutrient amounts are part of the constraints, not the objective function.
E. The number of constraints is a characteristic of the problem's structure, not the objective function.

QUESTION 6
A dietician is designing a meal plan to minimize cost while meeting certain nutritional requirements.  The meal plan consists of various foods, each with different costs and nutritional values. The dietician wants to determine the optimal quantities of each food to include in the meal plan. What type of linear programming problem is this?
A. A maximization problem
B. A transportation problem
C. An assignment problem
D. A minimization problem
E. A network flow problem

ANSWER: D

EXPLANATION:
A. While some linear programming problems involve maximization, the diet problem focuses on minimizing cost.
B. Transportation problems involve optimizing the flow of goods between sources and destinations. This is not the focus of the diet problem.
C. Assignment problems involve assigning tasks to agents in an optimal way. This is not directly related to the diet problem.
D. The diet problem aims to minimize the cost, making it a minimization problem.
E. Network flow problems deal with optimizing flow through a network. The diet problem does not have a network structure.

QUESTION 9
A dietician is formulating a diet plan using two foods, Food A and Food B. Food A costs $2 per unit and contains 10 units of nutrient X and 5 units of nutrient Y. Food B costs $3 per unit and contains 6 units of nutrient X and 12 units of nutrient Y. The minimum daily requirements are 60 units of nutrient X and 60 units of nutrient Y. Let $x_A$ and $x_B$ represent the number of units of Food A and Food B, respectively. What is the objective function for minimizing the cost of the diet?
A. Minimize $Z = 10x_A + 6x_B$
B. Minimize $Z = 5x_A + 12x_B$
C. Minimize $Z = 2x_A + 3x_B$
D. Minimize $Z = 60x_A + 60x_B$
E. Minimize $Z = 15x_A + 18x_B$

ANSWER: C

EXPLANATION:
A. This represents the total units of nutrient X.
B. This represents the total units of nutrient Y.
C. The objective function represents the total cost, which is $2x_A + 3x_B$.
D. This is incorrect; it uses the minimum requirements, not the costs.
E. This is incorrect; it is not related to the cost of the foods.
