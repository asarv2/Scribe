QUESTION 1
In $L^\infty$-regression, what is the primary objective of the optimization problem, and how does this objective differ from that of $L^2$-regression and $L^1$-regression?
A. To minimize the sum of absolute errors between the predicted and actual values, similar to $L^1$-regression.
B. To minimize the maximum absolute error between the predicted and actual values.
C. To minimize the sum of squared errors between the predicted and actual values, similar to $L^2$-regression.
D. To minimize the average absolute error between the predicted and actual values.
E. To maximize the minimum absolute error between the predicted and actual values.

ANSWER: B

EXPLANATION:
A. This describes the objective of $L^1$-regression, not $L^\infty$-regression.
B. The primary objective in $L^\infty$-regression is to minimize the maximum absolute error. This differs from $L^2$-regression, which minimizes the sum of squared errors, and $L^1$-regression, which minimizes the sum of absolute errors.
C. This describes the objective of $L^2$-regression, not $L^\infty$-regression.
D. Minimizing the average absolute error is a different objective, not directly related to $L^\infty$-regression.
E. Maximizing the minimum error is not the goal; the aim is to minimize the maximum error.

C. This is incorrect; $L^\infty$ is the least sensitive to outliers.
D. The $L^\infty$ norm focuses on the single largest error, making it relatively insensitive to outliers compared to $L^1$ and $L^2$. $L^1$ is more robust to outliers than $L^2$ because it only considers the magnitude of errors, not their squares.

QUESTION 8
Describe the geometric interpretation of the solution to the $L^\infty$-regression problem, and how does it differ from the geometric interpretation of the $L^2$-regression solution?
A. Both minimize the sum of squared distances to the fitted line.
B. $L^2$ minimizes the sum of squared distances, while $L^\infty$ minimizes the maximum distance.
C. $L^2$ minimizes the maximum distance, while $L^\infty$ minimizes the sum of squared distances.
D. Both minimize the maximum distance to the fitted line.
E. There is no geometric interpretation for $L^\infty$ regression.

ANSWER: B

EXPLANATION:
A. Incorrect; $L^2$ minimizes the sum of squared distances, but $L^\infty$ minimizes the maximum distance.
B. In $L^2$ regression, the solution minimizes the sum of squared vertical distances from the data points to the fitted line. In $L^\infty$ regression, the solution minimizes the maximum vertical distance from any data point to the fitted line.  This means the fitted line is contained within a band of width equal to twice the minimum maximum distance.
C. Incorrect; the descriptions of $L^2$ and $L^\infty$ are reversed.
D. Incorrect; only $L^\infty$ minimizes the maximum distance.
E. Incorrect; $L^\infty$ regression has a geometric interpretation.
