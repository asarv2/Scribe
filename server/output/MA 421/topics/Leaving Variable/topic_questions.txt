QUESTION 1
In the context of the simplex method, what is the primary criterion for selecting the leaving variable, and how does this choice ensure the feasibility of the next iteration?
A. The leaving variable is the variable with the smallest coefficient in the pivot column.
B. The leaving variable is selected based on the minimum ratio test, ensuring that all variables remain non-negative in the next iteration.
C. The leaving variable is the variable with the largest coefficient in the pivot row.
D. The leaving variable is chosen arbitrarily from the variables in the basis.
E. The leaving variable is the variable with the most negative coefficient in the objective function row.

ANSWER: B

EXPLANATION:
A. The smallest coefficient in the pivot column is not the criterion for selecting the leaving variable. This approach doesn't guarantee feasibility.
B. The minimum ratio test is the correct criterion for selecting the leaving variable.  It ensures that all variables remain non-negative after the pivot operation, maintaining feasibility.
C. The largest coefficient in the pivot row is not the criterion for selecting the leaving variable. This approach doesn't guarantee feasibility.
D. Arbitrarily choosing a variable from the basis is not a valid method for selecting the leaving variable, as it could lead to infeasible solutions.
E. The most negative coefficient in the objective function row is used to select the entering variable, not the leaving variable.
