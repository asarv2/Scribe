Theorem 10.2 states that for a set $S$ in $\mathbb{R}^m$, the convex hull of $S$, denoted $Conv(S)$, is equal to the set of all convex combinations of finitely many points from $S$. The proof starts by defining $H$ as the collection of all convex combinations of finitely many points from $S$. The goal is to show that $Conv(S) = H$. The first part of the proof, (i), aims to show that $Conv(S)$ is a subset of $H$, using the fact that $H$ is a convex set.  The underlining highlights the key statement of the theorem and the main goal of the proof. The notation "pf" stands for "proof," and "conv. comb." is short for "convex combination."
