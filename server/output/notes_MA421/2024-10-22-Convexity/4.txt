\section*{Theorem 10.1}
$S$ is convex $\iff$
it contains all conv. comb. of points in $S$

\textbf{Proof} $\Rightarrow$
Suppose $S$ is convex.
$n=2$: $z_1, z_2 \in S \implies \lambda z_1 + (1-\lambda) z_2 \in S$, $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 = 1$, $\lambda_1, \lambda_2 \ge 0$

$n=3$: $z_1, z_2, z_3 \in S \implies \lambda_1 z_1 + \lambda_2 z_2 + \lambda_3 z_3 \in S$, $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 + \lambda_3 = 1$, $\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \lambda_3 \ge 0$

$n=4$: $z_1, z_2, z_3, z_4 \in S \implies \lambda_1 z_1 + \lambda_2 z_2 + \lambda_3 z_3 + \lambda_4 z_4 \in S$, $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 + \lambda_3 + \lambda_4 = 1$, $\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \lambda_3, \lambda_4 \ge 0$

$(\lambda_1 + \lambda_2)( \frac{\lambda_1 z_1 + \lambda_2 z_2}{\lambda_1 + \lambda_2}) + \lambda_3 z_3 + \lambda_4 z_4 \in S$
$\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 \le 1$
$\lambda_1, \lambda_2 \ge 0$
$\lambda_3, \lambda_4 \ge 0$
