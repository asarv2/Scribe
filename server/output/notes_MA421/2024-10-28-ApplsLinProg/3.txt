\textbf{Description:}

This slide continues the ice cream production planning problem. It defines variables and a cost function for the linear programming model.  $X_i$ represents the production level in month $i$, and $S_i$ represents the surplus (or shortage) of ice cream at the end of month $i$.  The equation $X_i + S_{i-1} = d_i + S_i$ is a constraint, linking production, surplus, and demand.  The objective function $\min \sum_{i=1}^{12} 50|X_i - X_{i-1}| + \sum_{i=1}^{12} 20 S_i$ minimizes the total cost, which is the sum of the cost of changing production levels from month to month (represented by the absolute value term) and the cost of storing surplus ice cream (represented by the $S_i$ term).  The costs of changing production and storage are explicitly labeled.  This slide is likely presenting the mathematical formulation of the linear programming problem for the ice cream production planning.
