\begin{align*}

(P) \quad &\text{max} \quad x_1 + x_2 \\
&\text{s.t.} \quad 2x_1 + x_2 \le 14 \\
& \quad \quad -x_1 + 2x_2 \le 8 \\
& \quad \quad 2x_1 - x_2 \le 10 \\
& \quad \quad x_1, x_2 \ge 0
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
(D) \quad &\text{min} \quad 14y_1 + 8y_2 + 10y_3 \\
&\text{s.t.} \quad 2y_1 - y_2 + 2y_3 \ge 1 \\
& \quad \quad y_1 + 2y_2 - y_3 \ge 1 \\
& \quad \quad y_1 - 2y_2 + 2y_3 \ge 0 \\
& \quad \quad y_1, y_2, y_3 \ge 0
\end{align*}

\textbf{Description:}

This slide presents the primal (P) and dual (D) linear programming problems for the example from the previous slides.  The primal problem (P) is to maximize $x_1 + x_2$ subject to the constraints $2x_1 + x_2 \le 14$, $-x_1 + 2x_2 \le 8$, $2x_1 - x_2 \le 10$, and $x_1, x_2 \ge 0$. The dual problem (D) is to minimize $14y_1 + 8y_2 + 10y_3$ subject to the constraints $2y_1 - y_2 + 2y_3 \ge 1$, $y_1 + 2y_2 - y_3 \ge 1$, $y_1 - 2y_2 + 2y_3 \ge 0$, and $y_1, y_2, y_3 \ge 0$.  The slide explicitly shows the coefficients from the primal problem transformed into the dual problem.
